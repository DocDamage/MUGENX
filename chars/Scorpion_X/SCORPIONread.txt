  Controls
  --------
    Basic Controls
    --------------
      Scorpion has the classic MK layout, minus a block button.

      Notation:
      D - down
      F - forward
      U - up
      B - back
      a - button A
      b - button B
	c - button C
	x - button X
	y - button Y
	z - button Z
	Taunt - button Start


      DF means diagonally down-forwards, UB diagonally up-back,
      etc. If you see ab it means press button A and button B
      simultaneously. a/b means you can press either button A or
      button B.

      Use the direction keys to move SCORPION. Press down to crouch,
      and up to jump. SCORPION can double jump. 
	Hold the direction facing away from the opponent
      to guard. SCORPION can airblock

      In MK tradition, he can not run or back dash.  These ablities ARE programmed, just commented out...

    Standard Moves
    --------------
      Standing x     	- High Punch
	Standing a	   	- Low Punch
      	Pressing rapidly gets a combination punch

	Standing y     	- High Kick
	Standing b 	   	- Low Kick       
	Crouching x    	- INFAMOUS MK UPPERCUT
	Crounching a   	- Crouching Low Punch
	Crounching y   	- Crouching High Kick
	Crounching b   	- Crouching Low Kick 
  	Jumping up y,b 	- Jump Front Snap Kick
	Jumping forwards
	or backwards y,b	- Jumping side kick
	Jumping any 
	direction x,a	- Jumping Fist
	B + y			- Roundhouse Kick
	B + b			- Low Sweep
	(close in attacks)
	B or F + x 		- Forearm attack 
	y,b			- Knee
	F + a			- Throw (can be done in air)
	Taunt			- When I think about you, I nut myself... :^)
				  And other sillyness...
   
    Special Attacks
    ---------------
 	B,B + a		- Harpoon	
	F,F + a		- Low Harpoon
	D, DB, B + x	- Teleport Punch (can be done in air)
	D, DB, B + b	- Scissors Takedown
	x + a		- Harpoon Slash
	y + b		- Banana Peel Dodge
	
    FATALITIES
    ---------------
	F, B + x ,c		- Toasty (Inside sweep range or so)
	B, F + x, z		- Double Cut (One step away or so)
	B, F + a, z		- Impaler or Rolento's Meaner brother (Nearly full screen distance) 
	c			- Batman Fatality (about one step away...)
