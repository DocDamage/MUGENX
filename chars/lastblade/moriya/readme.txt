This has evidently become my personal forum for venting my
frustrations with the world of fighting games in general and/or
MUGEN and its "scene" in particular.  I've got a few gripes at the

time of this writing.  So don't be surprised if you see something
you don't like.  All righty.  Let's discuss "Capcom vs. SNK,"
forthcoming in arcades and for the DC.

While I'm sure this game will be fun to play, it's got several
strikes against it already in my book.  The lack of diversity in the
character roster, for one.  I'm not talking about in character

since the roster is HARDLY comprehensive.  It's not Capcom vs. SNK,

I HATE Street Fighter.  I got sick of the rehashing circa Super SF2

This news of "secret characters" restored a touch of hope, but that

                      ABOUT MINAKATA MORIYA

Moriya is the eldest of three foster children of no blood relation,
all adopted by a man named Gaisei.  The others were Yuki, four
years younger, and Kaede, five years younger.  Gaisei trained all
three in the art of the blade, though Yuki took more to a polearm
than a proper sword.

Moriya swiftly proved to be the superior to his foster siblings,
and soon came to surpass even his master in skill.  It was as if he
were born to weild a blade.  One day, in the year 1858, Moriya
found himself posessed of an inescapable sense of knowing who the
killer of his real parents was, and knew that the killer was in the
training hall.  For awhile, all was a hazy... and when he awoke, he
found himself standing over the body of his master, his foster
father.  With his dying breath, Gaisei told Moriya that he must
not seek vengeance on the murderer, which threw Moriya's already
panicked mind into further confusion... vengeance upon whom?

Kaede and Yuki returned at that point, and Kaede saw Moriya, the
foster brother whom he had loved dearly, standing over the body of
the only father he had ever known.  He went berserk, attacking
Moriya with his blade, who did not even try to dodge or block, so
overcome was he with grief and confusion.  Moriya took the hit in
silence.  Yuki attempted to stop them, but Moriya left the dojo
without a word, walking out of their lives for five years.

After five years, Kaede has trained diligently, and set off to
find Moriya and seek closure, whether it be in the form of
vengeance or redemption... and Yuki followed him, in order to
prevent further tragedy from befalling their ramshackle family.

Eventually, the true murderer of Gaisei was revealed to be Kagami
Shinnosuke.  In spite of this, Moriya never managed to again be the
older brother Yuki and Kaede so loved.

After the incident with Kagami, Moriya, cursing his inexperience,
once again left on a journey to train.  One day, half a year later,
Moriya felt an incredible desire to return home.  However, the
"home" to which he was drawn was not his own, but rather that of a
great evil.  With dark clouds rising, and unanswered questions
brimming within him, Moriya once again turns to Hell's Gate for
the truth.  And ultimately, reconciliation with his past.

                           Move List

Legend:
A    = Weak Slash
B    = Strong Slash
C    = Kick
Z    = Repel
qcf  = down, down-forward, forward
qcb  = down, down-back, back
dp   = forward, down, down-forward
hcb  = forward, down-forward, down, down-back, back
rqcb = back, down-back, down
df   = down-forward
f    = forward
b    = back
x3   = Perform stated motion three times
x4   = Perform stated motion four times

****** Miscellaneous ******
Throw                                               C+Z when close
Pursuit Attack                                      df + B

****** Special Moves ******
逸刀・朧・上段　    (Ittou Oboro Joudan)            qcb + A
逸刀・朧・中段　    (Ittou Oboro Chuudan)           qcb + B
逸刀・朧・下段      (Ittou Oboro Gedan)             qcb + C

Any of the Ittou Oboro (逸刀・朧) may be delayed by holding the
button down.  The ready state may be cancelled by pressing Repel.

逸刀・新月          (Ittou Shingetsu)               dp + A
逸刀・新月・裏      (Ittou Shingetsu Ura)           dp + B
逸刀・月影          (Ittou Tsukikage)               qcf + A (x3)
                                               -OR- qcf + B (x4)

帯刀・歩月          (Taitou Hougetsu [back])        rqcb + A
帯刀・歩月          (Taitou Hougetsu [forward])     rqcb + B
帯刀・歩月          (Taitou Hougetsu [behind])      rqcb + C

During the Taitou Hougetsu (帯刀・歩月), you may enter the Ittou
Oboro (逸刀・朧) upon reappearance by releasing the button and
pressing Weak Slash, Strong Slash, or Kick.

****** Desperation Move ******
活殺・十六夜月華    (Kassatsu Izayoi Gekka)         f, hcf + A+B
                                                    (hold to delay)

The Desperation Move requires either one super level, or life below
20%.  If life is below 20%, the super meter will not be drained.

****** Super Desperation Move ******
活殺・乱れ雪月花    (Kassatsu MidareSetsu Gekka)    f, hcf + B

The Super Desperation Move requires either two super levels, or one
super level with life below 20%.  If life is below 20%, the
super meter will only be drained one level instead of two.
