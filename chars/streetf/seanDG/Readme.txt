*Origin:

This char was ripped from "Street Fighter 3: 3rd Strike" game by Joram. All the efects were ripped from "Marvel vs Capcom", "Marvel vs Street Fighter", "Street Fighter Alpha 3" and "Super GEM Fighters" games by mine. The sounds were took from Ethan Lives's "Sean", from BMT(Brazil Mugen Team) page, from Ethan Lives's "Blaze Agent" and from the Sound Pack ripped by Joram too.

*About the creation:

First, I want to say that this Sean isn´t a perfect conversion of the "Street Fighter 3" game. He was made in "Marvel vs Capcom" style where I took the best from these two games for having a good compatibility of graphic and jogability. 
Some anims were edited by me like:
- 1 sprite in Air Throw move
- Air Burst move  

*Palletes:

- He has 12 palletes 
.button A: SF3 
.button B: SF3(2)  
.button C: SF3(3) 
.button X: MvsC 
.button Y: MvsC(2) 
.button Z: SF3(4)
.buttons Start + A: SF3(5) 
.buttons Start + B: SF3(6) 
.buttons Start + C: SF3(7)
.buttons Start + X: SF3(8)
.buttons Start + Y: SF3(9)
.buttons Start + Z: SF3(10)


*Attacks:

Basic:

- Commands:
.U - up   
.D - down
.F - forward
.B - backward
.X - light punch   
.Y - medium punch
.Z - high punch 
.A - light kick 
.B - medium kick 
.C - high kick  
.punches - any puches
.kicks - any kicks
.(c) - hold indicated button

- Super Jump:
.D, U

- Super Jump Forward:
.D, U + F

- Super Jump Backward:
.D, U + B

- Dash Forward: 
.F, F

- Dash Backward:
.B, B


Specials:

- Throw with hands: 
.(close to enemy) F or T + Z

- Throw with foots: 
.(close to enemy) F or T + C 

- Air Throw:
.(close to enemy) F or T + Z (on the air)

- Sean Pachiki
.F + Z

- Rolling Sobat
.F + C 

- Burst:
.D, DF, F + X (light)
.D, DF, F + Y (medium)
.D, DF, F + Z (high)
OBS: You can do on the air.

- Dragon Smash:
.F, D, DF + X (light)
.F, D, DF + Y (medium)
.F, D, DF + Z (high)

- Tornado Kick:
.D, DB, B + A (light)
.D, DB, B + B (medium)
.D, DB, B + C (high)
OBS: You can do on the air.

- Ryuubi Kyaku:
.D, DF, F + A (light)
.D, DF, F + B (medium)
.D, DF, F + C (high)

- Sean Tackle: 
.B, BD, D, DF, F + X (light)
.B, BD, D, DF, F + Y (medium)
.B, BD, D, DF, F + Z (high)
OBS: Hold punch until Sean to throw the opponent.

- Parry:  
.F - tap at the same time of enemy´s attack. (stand)
.B - tap at the same time of enemy´s attack. (crouch)
.F - tap at the same time of enemy´s attack. (air)
OBS: Special defence that you don´t lose life.

- Guard Push: 
.on Block, F + 2 socos ou 2 chutes
OBS: on Block = "(c)B" while enemy attacks.

- Recovery Roll: 
.B, DB, D + A or X (light)  
.B, DB, D + B or Y (medium)
.B, DB, D + C or Z (high)
OBS: Fast recover from enemy attacks. Do before HitGround.

- Alpha Counter: 
.on Block, B, DB, D + A + X (Sweep)  
.on Block, B, DB, D + B + Y (Ryuubi Kyaku)
.on Block, B, DB, D + C + Z (Shouryuu-Ken)
OBS: on Block = "(c)B" while enemy attacks.


Supers:
(you need at least 1 full powerbar(1000 of power) to do them)

- Hadou-Burst: 
.D, DF, F + 2 punches
OBS: You can do on the air.

- Shouryuu Cannon:
.D, DB, B + 2 punches
OBS: tap any punch rapidly to increase Hit.  

- Hyper Tornado:
.D, DB, B + 2 kicks


*Add-Ons:

- Combo Counter
OBS: Reduce Ryu´s attack damage in a combo attack.

- His taunt is an attack.
