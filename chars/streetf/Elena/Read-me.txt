    I'm glad to release Elena from SF3 because it was a hard and long work for me.
 I think I won't make another character as "detaillé" as her. "Surtout" if I have never
 played the game where he comes from.

    Ok, it's my first character and I would rather begin with a more simple fighter but
 I wanted to convert a char from Street Fighter's universe.
 At that time all chars from SFA3 where already converted then I started to work on her 
 in secret until she becomes something approximately playable even if someone have 
 announced his Elena WIP.
    After releasing my own WIP just beta, I realised that I was very far from the 
 accuracy I wanted to give. "les Critiques" have helped me to fix errors on her 
 (the faqs too) and with the time I updted each detail until what you have today.

 I hope you find her complete otherwise don't hesitate to send me your notes.

 Enjoy ! ;)

 ------------
= Moves List ==========================================================================
 ------------
 * Basic Moves *****
 Dash :              F, F
 Backflip :          B, B
 Long Jump :         D, U
 Parry :             F   (short time before being touched) / D   (when crouched)
 Quickstand :        D   (as soon as touched down)
 Air Recovery :      2P   (when air fall or over ground

 * Personal moves *
 Handstand Kick :    F + MP
 Bboy Kick :         F + MK
 Round Arch :        B + HK
 Slide Kick :        DF + HK
 Body Slam :         LP + LK
 Knee Overhead :     MP + MK
 One stand hand:     HP + HK
 Waker Kick :        2K   (when lie down)

 * Special moves **
 Lynx Tail :         B, D, DB + K                         EX-possible
 Mallet Smash :      F, DF, D, DB, B + P                  EX-possible
 Spin Scythe :       D, DB, B + K   (chain this 2 times)  EX-possible
 Rhino Horn :        B, DB, D, DF, F + K                  EX-possible
 Scratch Wheel :     F, D, DF + K                         EX-possible

 * Super Arts *****
 Spinning Beat :     D, DF, F, D, DF, F + K   (1/3 jauge) 
 Brave Dance :       D, DF, F, D, DF, F + P   (1/2 jauge)
 Healing :           D, DB, B, D, DB, B + P   (Total jauge)
