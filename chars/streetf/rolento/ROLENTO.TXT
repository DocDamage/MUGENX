Introduction
============

    This is Rolento from Capcom's Street Fighter Alpha 2/Alpha 3 combined for use
with Elecbyte's M.U.G.E.N Fighting Engine. As for the different -ISM modes in A3,
they have been combined to give you a more Alpha 2 feel.

===========

  * All special moves and attacks
  * Super moves - Take No Prisoner
		- Mine Sweeper	
  * 5 win poses and an intro animation
  * 3 throws/grab moves including an aerial one
  * Alpha/Zero Counters, Air Recover, Ground recovery roll, Grenade Taunt

= [ Basic Attacks worth mentioning... ] ==========================================================

  * Standing Strong / Medium Punch	(unblockable for ground to air)
		[ Y ]
  * Close Standing Fierce / Baton Uppercut
		back + [ Z ]
  * Standing Fierce / Hard Punch	(stand close for 2 hits)
		[ Z ]
  * Close Standing Forward / Knee Kick
		back + [ B ]
  ** Trick Rod				(avoids low attacks)
		forward + [ B ]

  ** Taunt			[ start ]

	- Rolento throws a grenade at his opponent. Short range, and explods into a puff of smoke.
	  The thing is that it can hit and it's unblockable. Does a little damage and burns the
	  victim. Good for opening up turtles who stays in the corner.

= [ Special Moves/Attacks ] =====================================================================

  * High & Quick Leap		[ tap Down, then Up or Back ] 

	- Rolento plants his rod into the ground and then pole vault himself forward or backwards
	  in a very long and shallow jump. During this jump, any attacks button presses will
	  result in normal aerial attacks.
	- This is your real Jump move. Rolento never do normal jumps, only super jumps so get
	  used to using this all the time. It has amazing speed and is a great way to start a
	  quick jump-in-combo or get out of one.
	- At the apex of the jump, it is high enough to get over most characters head so, even if
	  you missed an attack or is blocked, you will still land safely behind them. One of his
	  best combo starter would be doing his Strong punch attack during the jump. If done too
	  far off, you still get in a few hits of out 3 and then do some crouching attacks. If
	  done close, with good pratice, it is very possible to fly pass your opponent while
	  razing their heads for a good 3 hit and then landing behind them and continuing to 
	  perform wicked combos.

  * Patriot Circle		[ qcf + P ] / [ f, d, df, P ] (can be performed up to 3 times)

	- Rolento swings and spins his rod against your opponent for a maximum 11 hits (4/4/3).
	  This is his only combo-able special move so use it all the time after a combo-able
	  basic attack like crouching forward or from a close fierce attack.
	- Has 2 versions, if you start the Patriot Circle with a Jab punch, then Rolento will 
	  perform his Alpha 2 Patriot Circle style, start with the Strong punch and you'll get
	  his extra range ALpha 3 Patriot Circle but it does less damage compared his Alpha 2
	  Style Patriot Circle.

  * Stinger			[ dp + K ] / [ f, d, df, K ], then press any attack button

	- Rolento leaps into the air with knives in one hand. The range of his upwards jump
	  depends on the Kick you press. Pressing any button during the jump will make Rolento
	  spin and flip in mid-air and hurling a knife down. The button you use will determine
	  the angle of the knife to be thrown. Jab/Short will have a steepest descent while
	  Fierce/Roundhouse will have a much more horizontol descent, Strong/Forward will be
	  like the inbetween.
	- One of Rolento's best special move. Some of the uses are, quick jump over projectile
	  and returning a projectile of your own. Psyching your opponent thinking you are going
	  to throw a knife or do follow-ups like a slow close range knife from on top then do
	  a crouching slide or crouching fierce or throw a knife a screen apart then do a long
	  range jump and razing their heads with a jumping Strong attack.
	- One fault of Stinger is that the knifes can be canceled by any attacks, even basic
	  attacks but requires quite a but of timing and practice.

  * Mekong Delta Attack		[ PPP ], then press [ P ]

	- Pressing all three punches will cause Rolento to do a low back flip and pressing punch
	  again when he lands will make Rolento curl into a ball and rush forward quickly at his
	  opponent's feet. The range of his forward rush is determined by the strenght of the
	  punch button. If it hits, Rolento will then rebounds backwards before landing.
	- Usually overlooked but one of its best properties is that during the roll forward, it
	  passes thru all projectiles so if you think your opponent is going to throw a projectile
	  then do the backflip, if not, then the backflip is harmless and is a good get away move
	  since Rolento is a medium to long rang fighter.

  * Mekong Delta Air Raid	[ qcb + P ], then press [ P ]

	- Rolento tucks into a ball and roll backwards, pressing a punch button during the roll
	  makes him leap forward and attacking with a quicker jumping Fierce Punch attack.
	  The range of the backwards roll and forward leap is all determined by the strenght of
	  the punch button you choose.
	- Useful for running away from your opponent after a basic attack which is blocked only
	  to come back with a quick jumping attack. Also great if a projectile is throwned at
	  you during your roll.
	- Timing is important here since the hit-time of his jumping forward attack is small.
	  A very good move to do after jumping to close to your opponent and blocked, perform a 
	  throw move, almost gurantee to work all the time.

  * Mekong Delta Escape		[ qcb + K ], then hold forward or backwards + any button

	- Rolento leaps back very quickly until he reaches the corner of the screen behind him
	  then leaps off it and jumps forward. At this point, pressing any attack button will
	  cause Rolento to perform his basic jumping attacks. Holding forward or backwards during
	  the jump will allow you to control the velocity of his jump giving you full control of
	  the sky.
	- Most obvious is that you can use this move to quickly get away from your opponent and 
	  since the distant you can jump is controlable, this makes Rolento very dangerous. Holding
	  back will cause Rolento to stay back after the jump, usually to avoid projectiles or
          Dragon Punch-like attacks. Holding forward will cause Rolento to zoom way past your
	  opponent's head and landing on his back. Also, doing the Spike Rod special during his
	  jump will have different effects. You will be able to control the Spike Rod velocity and
	  land without any bounce. Go figure the uses.
	- The disadvantages of this amazing special move is that you cannot block or throw someone
	  during the jump.

  * Spike Rod			during jump, [ down + Medium Kick ], then hold forward or backwards

	- Rolento plants his rod beneath him during his jump. This will alter the velocity of his
	  jump usually shortening it. Upon landing, Rolento will bounce around using his rod like
	  a pogo stick forward or backwards depending on which button you hold down.
	- Not the most useful move but nevertheless, tricky move. Possible to use to abruptly stop
	  your character from a jumping too far or when you are expecting your opponent to to a
	  crouching attack when you land.
	- Possible to continously do pogo-sticking non-stop. Also, at the apex of your jump after
	  pogo-ing off the ground, Rolento can perform any aerial attacks with the right pratice
	  and timing.

  * Trick Landind		just before landing from a jump, press [ KKK ]
					** simplified to just [ KK ]

	- A quicker executioning version of Spike Rod. Not really useful, and it only bounces
	  backwards and you cannot follow up with an attack. Can and may be use as a tactic/skill
	  thingy.

= [ Super Moves/Attacks ] ==============================================================================

  * Take No Prisoner		[ qcf, qcf, punch ] ,	requires 1-3 levels of power bar

	- Rolento crouches low and sends a tripwire running across the floor towards his opponent.
	  If his opponent is hit by it, they will be dragged into the air and suspended from a hook
	  being held by an El Gato. Rolento let em hang for a little while then let them fall with
	  a flick on his finger on the wire.
	- Speed and damage of the tripwire depends on which level of super you choose. Move can only
	  be blocked low and it goes thru all projectiles. The drawback is that it cannot hit anyone
	  who is in air and only hit em when they are not in pain animation. In other words, can't combo
	  this super.
	- With speed comes advantages and disadvantages. If the move misses which can be pretty often,
	  Rolento loses alot of energy very quickly which is really not good. Rolento would agree with
	  you on this. So use this super wisely.
	- A possible move opener would to perform Steel Rain super then followed by the Take no Prisoner
	  super.

  * Steel Rain			[ qcf, qcf, kick ] ,	requires 1-3 levels of power bar

	- Rolento sends a rack of knifes into the air and knifes will rain on your opponent a second
	  later.
	- Slow and vulnerable, only use when opponent is far away or slow moving or down on the floor.

  * Mine Sweeper		[ qcb, qcb, punch ] ,	requires 1-3 levels of power bar

	- Rolento rolls back quickly and throw out multiple grenades as he makes his escape. Halting at
	  the end of the roll just in time for fireworks.
	- Great anti-air super, unblockable in for opponents in air. Good damage. But as usual, it's not
	  combo-able and you need to anticipate jump-ins.
	- Bad recovery for Rolento is the super is blocked and this super drags the opponent to Rolento.
	  Which means, Rolento might eat a super if he misses.

= [ Throws/Grab Moves ] ================================================================================

  * Carnal Carry		ground throw, when close hold [backwards/forwards] + [ PP ]

	- Rolento grabs his opponent and tosses em forward using his baton.

  * Deadly Package		ground throw, when close, hold [backwards/forwards] + [ KK ]

	- Rolento hops behind his opponent and hangs a string of grenades around the victim and
	  jumping away quickly just before the grenades blow up.

  * Deadly Package (air)	air throw, when close, hold [backwards/forwards] + [ KK ]

	- same as the ground Deadly Package throw but works in air and looks great. Can be performed
	  during a high & quick leap.

= [ Alpha Counters ] ================================================================================

  * Punch Alpha Counter		while blocking, perform [ b, db, d, P ] requires 1 level of energy

	- Rolento does the final round of his Patriot Circle. It does the regular 3 hits but it does
	  more damage if it all connects and sends the opponent spinning & flying across the screen.
	- Great range and not affected by most projectiles. Be careful if done too close, as Rolento
	  may go too far forward, thus missing the opponent.

 * Kick Alpha Counter		while blocking, perform [ b, db, d, K ] requires 1 level of energy

	- Rolento hops across his opponent and quickly lands behind them if done close enough.
	  Great escape move if cornered and at the same time, Rolento can retaliate from behind.

= [ Custom V-Ism Combo ] =================================================================================

  * Custom V-Ism Combo		either air or ground, press [P+K], requires 1 level of energy and up

	- Allows Rolento to chain and cancel most moves into others allowing massive combo and juggles.
	  Supers are not allowed during this mode. Will end as soon as Rolento is hit or power runs out.
	  Also, Rolento cannot block in this mode. 
	- Activation of CVC at close range will stun the victim and pulls em closer if they are not
	  guarding low. Also, there's a short invulnerability during start up. More so in the air version.
	- If victim is knocked off his feet, he cannot air recover so juggle as much as possible.
	- Here are some combo starters : 
		1) perform Patriot Circle x 3 and cancel into a close fierce punch or roundhouse kick then
		   cancel into super jump and juggle as you like.
		2) Use crouching roundhouse sweep to knock em down and then quickly use close fierce punch
		   to knock em up and juggle as you like. (CVS2 ^^)
