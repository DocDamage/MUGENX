  Controls
  --------

    Basic Controls
    --------------
      D - down
      F - forward
      U - up
      B - back
      x - button X (light punch)
      y - button Y (strong punch)
      z - button Z (rolling)
      a - button A (light kick)
      b - button B (strong kick)
      c - button C (not use)

    Standard Movement
    -----------------
      U              - Jump
      UF             - Jump forwards
      F              - Walk forwards
      DF             - Crouch
      D              - Crouch
      DB             - Crouch or low guard
      B              - Walk backwards or high guard
      UB             - Jump back
      F, F           - Forward dash
      B, B           - Back dash
      x+a            - Recovery (when falling)
       
    Basic Standing Attacks
    ----------------------
      x              - L. Punch
      y              - M. Punch
      z              - S. Punch
      a              - L. Kick
      b              - M. Kick
      c              - S. Kick

    Basic Crouching Attacks
    -----------------------
      x              - L. Punch
      y              - M. Punch
      z              - S. Punch
      a              - L. Kick
      b              - M. Kick
      c              - S. Kick

    Basic Air Attacks
    -----------------
      x              - L. Punch
      y              - M. Punch
      z              - S. Punch
      a              - L. Kick
      b              - M. Kick
      c              - S. Kick

    Throws
    ------
      F/B + y/z             - Deadly Throw
      F/B + b/c             - Dead Rise 

    Special Attacks
    ---------------

      B Charging F + Punch                  - Psycho Crusher

      B Charging F + Kick                   - Double Knee Press

      D, DB, B + Punch                      - Psycho Impact

      F, D, DF + Punch                      - Psycho Vanish

      D Charging U + Punch                  - Devil Reverse

      D Charging U + Kick                   - Head Press

      Punch until Head Press                - Somersault Skull Diver

    Hyper Attacks
    -------------
      
      D, DF, F, D, DF, F + Punch          - Heart Break Despair

      D, DF, F, D, DF, F + Kick           - Knee Press Nightmare
