-----------

                           SP+FP

                      FK+RK

                             FK+RK

Cannon Spike - DP + K
Thrust Kick - QCB + K (IN AIR)
Spin Knuckle - HCF + P
Hooligan Combo - HCB + K / Follow with P in close for Hooligan Slam
                           Follow with K in close for Hooligan Hurracanrana
Hooligan Counter - HCB + P
Recovery Roll Forward - HCF + P (When hiting the ground)
Recovery Roll Backwards - HCB + P (When hiting the ground)
Taunt - Start
