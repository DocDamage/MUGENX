Name: Urien
Origin: Street Fighter 3: 3rd Strike
Last Updated: November 14, 2005

- Well, the only thing I'm concerning about is the timing for now.


Urien has:
All of his super art
- Aegis Reflector: D, F, D, F, any punch (two punch for the upper Aegis)

Reflect most helper move if it has attribute of a projectile.

- Jupiter Thunder : D, B, D, B, any punch

Does 5 hits.

- Tyrant Punish : D, F, D, F, any kick

Does 5 hits.

All of his moves
- Violence Knee Drop: Charge D, for two sec. then U any kick(Two Kick for EX)

Each button determine how far he will go. Weak Kick goes a short distance and Fierce Kick goes far. EX version is homing and also hit twice.

- Dangerous Head Butt: Charge D, for two sec. then U any punch(Two Punch for EX)

The only difference between the button is that the stronger the attack button the more damage it does and has some velocity change. EX version goes farther and hits twice.

- Metallic Sphere: D, F, any punch(Two Punch for EX)

Each button determine the angle for the projectile. Weak Punch goes straight forward and Fierce Punch goes diagonally. EX version only goes straight but hits twice. 

- Chariot Tackle: Charge B, for two sec. then F any kick (Two Kick for EX)

Each button determine how far he tackle. Weak Kick goes shortest and Fierce Kick goes farther. EX version goes far and hits twice.

Urien also has sound from Capcom Fighting Evolution(Capcom Fighting Jam).
