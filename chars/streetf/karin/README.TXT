----------------------------------------------------------Gameplay Overview.

Karin is still true to her original SFZ3 form. Vulnerabilities, strength
and speed are quite exact to her original incarnation. Only difference
is her air combo and chaining ability, where you can choose to have
these abilities or not.

On Original style, Karin loses all her dashes, low jumping, certain
sound effects, and chaining. But this reproduces her original feel in
the game.

Crossover Karin moreso diversifies her attacks, making her more advanced
and even fun to play than the original. For instance, she can learn a
single attack of an enemy, and more.

To choose Karin with original flavor, press start while selecting her.

NORMAL MOVES & THROWS ----------------------------------------------------

- Elegant Kick (Fwd+k)
Karin dances, her leg hitting down. Attacks like this one must be
blocked high.

- Kanzuki Otoshi (fwd/back + 2 punch buttons) [can be done in the air]
Karin grabs the enemy, and with her own gravitational force, pushes her
foe down to the dirt.

- Knee Grab (fwd/back + 2 kick buttons)
Karin grabs the enemy, and bashes him/her with her own knee. Note that I
added a special coding for people shorter than Karin. :p If your
character does not look right when Karin kneebashes her, adjust your
headpos and midpos.

SPECIAL MOVES -----------------------------------------------------------

- Hou Shou (dp + p)
Palm strike that pummels the enemy! >:D Button variates her timing of
the attack. A nice combo-ender to pummel your enemy!

- Guren Ken (qcf + p)
Karin lunges forward with one fist. From this attack, you can choose any
of other chainable attacks. Normally, pressing another punch button
during Guren Ken will do another palm strike, and then to Hou Shou.
All of the attacks below will work during the first and second chain
duration of Guren Ken.

 - Mujin Kyaku (any kick button)
 - Daisokubarai (down + any kick button)
   Looks like her Crouch HK, but sliding.
 - Ressen Chou (downforward + any punch button)
 - Ressen Ha (up + any kick button)
 - Yasha Gaeshi (back, plus any punch button to counter high attacks,
   kick for low attacks.)
 - Hou Shou (forward + punch button)

- Ressen Ha (qcf + k)
A spinning punch maneuver that hits high. Great to use against turtlers.
:P. Button determines distance. The second hit must be blocked high.

- Ressen Chou (rdp + p)
An elbow attack. Press another button for a second strike. Yet another
combo ender! :p This attack may be quick enough to go by as a counterattack.

- Mujin Kyaku (dp + k)
A double split anti-air kick. Yum!

- Yasha Gaeshi Joudan (qcb + p) / Yasha Gaeshi Gedan (qcb + k)
Karin awaits the attack of an enemy, and counters, accordingly. Do you
know her second function of this move in Crossover Mode??

- Arakuma Inashi (360 motion + k)
Karin knees her enemy, and flips 'em! Ehe!

SUPERS -------------------------------------------------------------------

- Shinpikaibyaku (qcfx2 + p)
Karin does various punching motions, which pummels the enemy. great for
comeback attacks!

- Kououken (qcfx2 + k)
Karin spins upward! A dangerous anti-air move. This move can juggle
enemies!

- Shin Guren Ken (qcbx2 + p)
Karin's own version of Yun's You Hou. Another table-turning attack!
