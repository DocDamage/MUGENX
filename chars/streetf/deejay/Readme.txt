Special Moves:
--------------
Air Slasher - Hold B, then F, P
Rolling Sobat - Hold B, then F, K
Machine Gun Uppercut - Hold D, then U, P
		Rapidly press P for more hits.
JackKnife Maximum - Hold D, then U, K
JackKnife Maximum Counter - When blocking, 2P or 2K

Super Moves:
------------
super moves marked with '*' are hard to pull off, so I
made alternate commands so you can do the move easier.
------------
*Sunrise Theme - Hold DB, Then DF, DB, UF, P
	(easy) - QCFx2, P
Sobat Carnival - Hold B, Then F, B, F, K
*Climax Beat - Hold DB, Then DF, DB, UF, K
	(easy) - QCBx2, K
Super Air Slasher - Hold B, Then F, B, F, P
Super Sobat Kick - QCFx2, K (lvl3 only)
	Press P when opponent comes close for extra hit.
