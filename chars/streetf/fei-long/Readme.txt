Fei-Long SFA3: Version 1.6a
----------------------
Mugen Version: 2001.04.14
Author: N64Mario (N64Mario84@hotmail.com)
Percent Complete = 90%
Released: 7/24/02
----------------------
EX Mode: To use EX mode, hold start at character select screen.

Notes for EX Mode:
------------------
Slightly different stance
Does not have close normal attacks
Hold back, press attack button for close attacks
Blue flame instead of an orange flame
Fwd+2P throw has an extra Rekkaken combo
Can do Rekkaken up to 5 times instead of 3
Rekka Shinken is extended for more hits
Ryu Yassai has 3 different levels instead of 1

Whats in this version:
----------------------
12 Palettes
Required animations
All basic movement
All basic attacks
All basic close attacks
Throwing
Special Moves
Super Moves
2 Intros, 2 Taunts
2 Win poses
Recovery Roll
Alpha Counter

Special Moves:
--------------
Rekkaken - QCF, P (up to 3 times)
Shienkyaku - B, D, DB, K
Rekkukyaku - HCF, K
Flaming Dragon Fist - Hold B, Then F, P
Rekkaken Uppercut Combo - P+P (standing still)
Flying Kick - K+K (standing still)

Bruce Lee Moves:
----------------
One-Inch Fist - B, F, P
Dragon Stomp - Dx2, K
Flying Dragon Kick - F, D, DF, K

Alpha Counter:
-------------
Shienkyaku - While Blocking, 2K

Super Moves:
------------
Rekka Shinken - QCFx2, P
Ryu Yassai - QCBx2, P
Ryu Shin Yassai - QCBx2, P (Max Distance)
Shien Renkyaku - QCBx2, K
Shin Dragon Stomp - Dx4, K
Ultimate Combo - B, F, B, F, P

EX Super Moves:
---------------
Flame Punch - QCBx2, LP
Ryu Yassai - QCBx2, MP
Ryu Shin Yassai - QCBx2, HP
