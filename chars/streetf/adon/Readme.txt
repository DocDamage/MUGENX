*==================*=============*
| Adon By Pioupiou | Version 1.0 |
*==================*=============*


	12/25/2000 - V 1.0 : 100 % completed


	Build time : about 1 month 


Hi here here is Pioupiou. Here is my lastest character, Adon coming from Sfa series.
I based this character on Ownko's Adon, but i think this is more than an update
so i release it as a complete char (To be honest, 80 % of the sprite and sound rip is coming 
directly from this adon, even if i had to edit all his sprites to fill all the "holes"
that were on the char, everything else have been made or edited/remade) but big thanks to Ownko.
Well i think he has everything that he has in SFA 1, 2 or 3, by example most of the basics
specials and the throws were ripped from Sfa1, but the gethits and the gameplay is more
SFA3 style.
The gameplay so should be like in Sfa3, except for the throws (i rather Sf2 throw system than Sfa3 one),
you might find him a bit fast, but i only play SFa3 in Turbo 2 mode ;) (Don't worry he is well balanced Imho)
Well enjoy this char and remember :
	
	Jaguar will kicks everybody's ass ! ;)

*------------*
| Moves list |
*------------*

Here is the list of the moves :  

- Basics :

	a : Low Kick

	b : Medium Kick

	c : High Kick

	x : Low Punch

	y : Medium Punch

	z : High Punch

	Start : Taunt
	
	Forward + y : Dash punch  
		
		Adon does a dashing double punch hit.
	
	Forward or Back + any punch : Punch Throw
	
	Forward or Back + any kick : Kick Throw

- Specials : 

	Forward, Down, DownForward + any kick : Rising Jaguar

		Adon will do a double knee uppercut.

	Back, Down, DownBack + any kick : Jaguar Kick

		Adon will jump and do a quick and powerfull rolling kick.

	Forward, DownForward, Down, DownBack, Back + any kick : Jaguar Tooth

		Adon will jump back to the screen then he will do a flying kick.

- Hypers : 

	Down, DownForward, Forward, Down, DownForward, Forward + any kick : Jaguar Revolver - need 1,2 or 3 level of power

		Adon will do a strong Jaguar kick then he will combo hit with fast Jaguar Kick(s).

	Down, DownForward, Forward, Down, DownForward, Forward + any punch : Jaguar Varied Assault - need 1,2 or 3 level of power

		Adon will do a dashing punch combo and finish by a powerfull punch + knee uppercut.

- Others : 


	Forward or Back + any two kick; while falling in air : Recovery roll

		Adon will not bump on the ground but rolling on it a go far/near the opponent.

	Forward, Forward, + any kick ;or; Down, DownBack, Back + any kick; while guarding a hit : Alpha/Zero Counter

		Adon will counter attack. This move will take off a few power if it hits.

	Forward, Forward, + any punch ;or; Down, DownBack, Back + any punch; while guarding a hit : Evade roll

		Adon will avoid the attack by rolling away from the opponent so you can counter attack like you want.
