Hello, I am Wild Ali, and this is my first major release for MUGEN.
I am a big fan of Q.  I always thought that the guy had potential, but 
got shortchanged with what we computer nerds call the "nerf".  Yes, the
guy was cool-looking, but he was too slow, too laggy, and too awkward

 Storyline:
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Q, from a glance, is just a simple man dressing a suit and trenchcoat, 
with matching fedora to boot.  His most striking feature, visually, is 
his metallic mask, which is made to look like a face portraying a bland
expression, save the eyes - the "eyes" are actually holes cut into the 
mask, shining a light from whatever lies beneath.  His initial look 
harkens back to that of the Hollywood Private Detective, save for the 
fact that he is far more sinister than your typical gumshoe.  His 
actions and movements, while not very mechanical in nature, seem to 
radiate the feel of a man trapped within a machine.  You can constantly
hear a rasping wheeze come from him, as if whatever may lurk beneath
the mask is struggling to breath through mechanical means.

 Q Overview:
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Q has a terrible reputation for being a totally worthless character.
Japanese players have shown us that this is not the case.  In 3rd 
Strike, Q is a absolute BEAST in the right hands, but let's not kid 
ourselves.  He is at an extreme disadvantage against 3rd Strike 
top-tier.  I have attempted to fix most of his really glaring flaws, 
such as a lack of combos, useless Overhead and Low Dash attacks, and 
a really sucky super in Total Destruction.

And now...notes on Q's various moves:

 * Rush Leg and Head Attack (Dash Straight) have been sped up 
   considerably.  They also have added invincibility on startup too.
   They still have their unique advantages and disadvantages for each, 
   but now all of his dash punches are useful, not just the EX attack.
 * High Speed Barrage (Rapid Swipes) now moves slightly forward when
   executed and has startup invincibility.
 * Deadly Double Combo has been toned down.
 * Total Destruction has been reworked.  Instead of working like Shuma-
   Gorath's Chaos Dimension, it's now just a command grab.  It can't be
   blocked standing at all, but it CAN be blocked crouching or while in
   the air.  If this move is executed on you, you must duck or jump to
   avoid it, but the move still comes out fast enough to be effective.
   Also of note is that you also take damage from the blast, even
   though it'll never kill you.  TD is still Q's most powerful super.

 Special Moves:
--------------------------------

Rush Head Attack - Hold back 2 seconds, then forward X/Y/Z (Hold down
                   button for overhead sweep)

Rush Leg Attack - Hold back 2 seconds, then forward, A/B/C

Rush Head Attack (EX) - Hold back 2 seconds, then forward and any two
                        punch buttons (X/Y/Z)

High Speed Barrage - D, DB, B, X/Y/Z

High Speed Barrage (EX) - D, DB, B, any two punch butons (X/Y/Z)

Capture and Deadly Blow - Half-circle backwards, and A/B/C

Command Throw 1 (Knee Slam) - Press LK and LP near opponent.

Command Throw 2 (Ground Slam) - Hold F/B and press LK and LP near
                                opponent.

--------------
 Super Moves:
---------------------------------

Critical Combo Attack - D, DF, F, D, DF, X/Y/Z

Deadly Double Combo - D, DB, B, D, DB, X/Y/Z

Total Destruction - B, D, DB, D, DB, A/B/C
