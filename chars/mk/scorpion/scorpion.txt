-MOVES-
High Spear - B, B, LP 
Low Spear -  F, F, LP
Teleport - D, DB, B, HP (in air also)
Flame - Hold HP ... release HP
Skull Rise - D, DB, B, LK
Leg Takedown - D, DB, B, c
Ninja Slide - HK+LK
Air Throw - F+LP (in air)

-SUPER MOVES-
Super Flame Breathe - D, F, D, F, HP 
Triple Skull Rise - D, B, D, B, LK
Skull Rise Kombo - D, F, D, F, HK
Phoenix - D, F, D, F, LK
Fire Dragon - Hold LP...release LP

-FATLITIES-

Toasty - HP (anywhere far)
Lava Pour - LK (anywhere far)
Brutality - HK (anywhere far)
Hell Hand - HK (sweep)
Hell Rain - Hold Z...release Z (far)
Animality - Hold C...release C (anywhere far)
Face Flame - LP (anywhere far)
Soul Steal - C (sweep)
Fire Eruption - C (1/2 screen)
Lava Pond Punch - z (then hold forward) (close)
Pit Knockoff - z (then hold Down) (close)
Spike Uppercut - z (then hold Up) (close)

Jump Kick -> Air Teleport -> Spear -> Skull Rise Kombo -> Spear ->Roundhouse (then teleport -> uppercut)

*NOTE: To make Scorpion the typical MUGEN character size, open his
.CNS file and scroll to where it says:

[Size]
xscale = 1   
yscale = 1

Replaces the 1's with .9, then SAVE!
