Notes

Well I know I took my time releasing Dong Hwan but I guess the 
wait is now over, Thank The Lord. Hwan is by far my most accurate 
and high quality character so far. 

So another Kim Character!!! Yep I am a real big Kim Family Fan!!!
Like I have said before! maybe I just love Tae Kwon Do, who knows? 
In this version everything from Garou has been implemented, this
means he now has his throw, his intro vs Jae Hoon from Garou, and 
1 or 2 sounds which were missing b4.

By The Way I am aware that the other Palettes aren't the nicest
palettes in the world but they are the actual palettes from Garou,
so don't blame me for that one or E-Mail me about it.

-----------------------------------------------
Whats New
-----------------------------------------------
Well since the last version, I have added the intro Vs Jae Hoon, the 
throw, and an original special move. This special move is the Hienzan
It is kind of like the Kim Family version of the Flash Kick, Kaphwan
had it, Jae Hoon had it, but Dong Hwan didn't although he did have
the Kuu Sa Jin which Jae Hoon does not have. Anyway in this version of 
Hwan u can now perform the Hienzan, a pretty effective move, and also 
good for comboing into from other attacks. 
Also some velocities for when Hwan is coming down from the air have
been fixed so that Hwan looks like he is accelarating down instead
of floating.
Last But not least I have improved the P.Power Raimei Dan so that it 
is the same as in Garou, so now u can charge the super for a sec so 
that u can get up to 4 hits. I apologise for not having implemented 
this before but I didn't actually know u could do this in Garou until 
after I had released the 99% version.

Oh and before I forget, a few ppl bugged me for a Capcom style button 
layout. If u want to use the Alternate cmd file which will do this, 
you'll have to modify the Dong.def file like this.
--Before--		
cmd     = Dong.cmd     ;Garou Command set
;cmd     = DongAlt.cmd     ;Capcom Command set

--After-
;cmd     = Dong.cmd     ;Garou Command set
cmd     = DongAlt.cmd     ;Capcom Command set

Make sure u do this correctly otherwise u could mess things up, if u have 
any trouble just E-Mail me.

TOP Mode & Just Defend
------------------------------------------------

To use the TOP Mode (Tactical Offensive Position) system:
Depending on the button you use to select, Hwan's T.O.P. bar
will be in either the upper, middle or lower part of the
lifebar.  When in this mode, he will begin flashing white, and
slowly gain life, up until the highest point of the TOP zone.
---- 
Key
----

Button      TOP section     Palette
-------------------------------------
  A           upper            1
  B           middle           1
  C           lower            1
  X           upper            2
  Y           middle           2
  Z           lower            2
Start+A       upper            3
Start+B       middle           3
Start+C       lower            3
Start+X       upper            4
Start+Y       middle           4
Start+Z       lower            4


To use the Just Defended system:
Block at the last moment, and Hwan will flash blue and gain 
back a tiny bit of life.

------------------------------------------------
Moves List
------------------------------------------------
I have made Hwan's button layout to be exactly the same as in Garou, 
this is partly so that he is as accurate to the original as possible,
also because I like the button layout in Garou

A = Weak Punch
B = Weak Kick
X = Strong Punch
Y = Strong Kick
C = Taunt

[Special Moves]

Overhead attack - A+B

Roundhouse Kick - Down+A+B

Raimei Zan - QCB+B or Y

Kuu Sa Jin - F,D,F+A or X

Hishou Kyaku - in air, Down+B or Y

Shiden Kyaku - QCF+B or Y

Ashi Binta - HCB+X

Hienzan(original) - ChargeDown(for a sec), Up+B or Y 

[TOP Attack]

Can only be performed in Top Mode - X+Y

[Super Moves]


[S.Power] - Can be performed When you have 1 level or higher

Super Dong Hwan Kyaku - In Air QCF,QCF+A

Raimei Dan - QCF,QCF+B 


[P.Power] - Can be performed When you have 2 levels or higher

Super Dong Hwan Kyaku - In Air QCF,QCF+X

Raimei Dan - QCF,QCF+Y (Hold "Y" to charge the super for another sec to get up to 4 hits as opposed to just 2)

Ore-sama Hou-Ou Kyaku - QCF,QCF,QCF+X
