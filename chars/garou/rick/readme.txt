--Instructions--

Simply unzip the files into the required folder of your choosing. I.E. '/chars/rick'.
Next add the line. 'rick,stages/stage0.def' to the select.cfg file in the mugen data
directory. (Stage0.def is the default, i believe, but it can be subsituted for 
any stage of your choosing). Rick's background will be released soon.

--BreakShots--

All Break Shots are now included.
They can be performed when blocking, similare to garou when a just defend is done.
The Divine Blast and Blazing Sunbrust can be executed at a faster although less 
powerful rate, while blocking. good for breaking combos. 


--Progressive Strikers--

Each stiker can be called in whenever so desired as long as the power meter is greater than 250.
Progressive Strikers are called when the corresponding button is pressed when the final move of
the last stiker hits. So in this way up to 3 stikers can be called in succession for one painful combo.
