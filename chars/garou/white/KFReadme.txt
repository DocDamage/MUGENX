Background Info: 
================

Move List:
==========
White is a 3 button character that attacks mainly with his cane, though his attacks can be made different depending on
directional key. Note his attacks tend to have high priority, and will do block damage if they hit.

Basic:
------
X:     Straight 2-Hit Cane
P+X:   Step Rod
DF+X:  Right Slash Rod

Y:     Upper 2-Hit Cane

Z:     Hell to Heaven Cane
F+Z:   Left Slash Rod
DF+Z:  Double Slash Rod
