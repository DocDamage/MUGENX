 Installation
+++++++++++++++
 DownLoad 1 file named "Marco.zip".
 The "Marco.zip" contains 21 files shown below.
  Marco.air		Marco7.act
  Marco.cmd		Marco8.act
  Marco.cns		Marco9.act
  Marco.def		Marco10.act
  Marco.sff		Marco11.act
  Marco1.act		Marco12.act
  Marco2.act            Marco.snd
  Marco3.act		ReadMe_J.txt (In Japanese)
  Marco4.act		ReadMe.txt (In English, this file)
  Marco5.act		Skill.zip (Containes commands)
  Marco6.act

 Unzip a file then, put these files into 1 folder named "Marco".
 Then Put "Marco" folder into your Mugen "charas" folder.

 Ex.)
   muk00101-----charas
              :    |____Marco
              :    :       |____Marco.air
              :    :       :
              :    :       :
              :    :       :

 Add this line in your "select.cfg" in "data" folder.

 Marco, stages/******.def

 Replace ****** with your favorite background name.

 Ok, run your Mugen then the Marco's face is added your character
 select screen. Let's enjoy !!!

 Featuer
++++++++++++++++++
 / It is possible that you can choose a kind of Top Mode.
   Top A ... Life = (1000,1500]
   Top B ... Life = (500,1000]
   Top C ... Life = (0,500]
   < How to choose a kind of Top Mode>
    Top A ... CharacterColor 1 & 4 & 7 & 10
    Top B ... CharacterColor 2 & 5 & 8 & 11
    Top C ... CharacterColor 3 & 6 & 9 & 12
    The pallete ording is ,
    X  Y  Z
    1  2  3
    4  5  6
    A  B  C
    Holding start while selecting will add 6 to the palette number.
