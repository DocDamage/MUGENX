Commands List
==============================================================================================
---Specials---

Gama Tornado > F, DF, D, DB, B + Punch

Gama Toss > 30$B, F + kick (press again for a vertical gama toss)

Zangief Helper > X+A

---Supers---
Gama Wave > D, DF, F + any two punch combo

Gama Crush > D, DB, B + any two punch combo

Gama Slam > D, DF, F + any two Kick combo
