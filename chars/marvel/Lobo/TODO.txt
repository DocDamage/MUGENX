What's done:
----------------------
SPECIALS:
Mr. Hook (QCB + P) -- Lobo uses his old friend Mr. Hook to get "up close and personal" with his opponent
Frag! (QCF + P) -- 3-shot gun blast
Rot Gut (D,DB,B + K) -- Lobo takes a swig from his bottle of cheap rotgut liquor, gaining a random
                        powerup (but watch out! It might sometimes make him sick, and it leaves him wide open to attack!)

HYPERS:
Main Men (QCB + KK) -- Lobo slashes his wrist to draw some blood, which grows into a couple of Lobo-clones.
                        These guys mimic his moves until they run out of energy & die (or get fragged)
