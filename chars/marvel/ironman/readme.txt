Iron Man version1.0c by Gou-San

-Introduction
Hello friends! Here it is one more character made by the only Gou-San! He is Iron Man, but not just Iron Man! He came with Special Partners from Marvel vs Capcom! He has MvsC2 Gameplay and combos (at least I think so!). The Special Partners are just like the original game. Superior Tech!
