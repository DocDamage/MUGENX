Gameplay Overview
--------------------------------------------
She plays like a Mix of her XMvSF and MvC2 incarnations. It's all about the power stealing here, although the MvC2 powerups are available by using the Kiss of Death super.

 
=====================|Moves List|==========================

F=forward
B=back 
U=up
D=Down
x=weak punck
y=medium punch
z=strong punch
a=weak kick
b=medium kick
c=strong kick


Normal Moves----------------------------

Dash (You can dash in the air) - Double tap F

BackDash - Double tap B

Lift and Smack - B or F+y/z when close
-Can be done in air. 

Counter Attack - B,DB,D+attack while blocking
-Costs 1 power lever to do. Punch and Kick do different attacks.

Guarding Repel - two punches while blocking
-Useful for getting breathing space.

Roll Recover - B,DB,D+kick while falling from opponent's attack
-Use this to roll and instantly get up, as opposed to being vulnerable by laying on the ground. 


Special Moves---------------------------

Rushing Assault - D, DF, F, punch
-Stronger the button, the more punches she delivers

Rising Assault - F, D, DF, punch
-Tap the punch buttons for more hits. Get up to 6 hits with fierce.

Power Dive - F, D, DF, kick
-Can be cancelled early like in MvC2 by pushing kick. NEW! Can be used in air for instant dive.

Power Drain - D, DB, B, kick
-The bread and butter of Rogue's gameplay. Doesn't do much damage, but cannot be blocked. If fighting against a supported character, you gain a special and super from them. If the character is not supported, a power will be selected at random.

Super Moves-----------------------------

Goodnight, Sugah - D, DF, F,2 punches at level one
-Rushes at opponent and delivers the smackdown!

Kiss of Death - D, DB, B, 2 punches at level one
-Not very strong but can't be blocked, if it connects, you gain a special power.
-POWER UP-Hits are stronger
-SPEED UP-Move faster
-DEFENSE-Less damage
-EXCEL(New!)-Combo anything
-ARMOR-I can't stop me..... er, I mean, your opponent can't hit you out of your attacks.
-HEALING-Restores a little bit of life

Stolen Super - D,DF,F, 2 kicks at level 1
-Use the super move you've stolen


The Power Stealing System_____________________________________________

When you begin, you'll have no power. In order to get a power, you must connect with a Power Drain (or a move that contains a Power Drain). A picture will appear showing you the power you receive. These pictures are usually blue, but sometimes they're a adifferent color. When it's a different color, it means that you'll receive extra goodies with that stolen power.

BLUE-Nothing Special
RED-Can do an extra Level 3 Super by using (D,DB,B,2k), (F,D,DF,2k), or (x,x,F,a,z) Depends on character.
GOLD-Can use a special Striker by pressing y+b and a level 3 Variable Cross with (D,DB,B,z+c)
FLASHING-RED and GOLD COMBINED.....very rare

Stolen Powers last between rounds and matches..... keep that in mind. ;)
