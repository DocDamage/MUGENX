 How to play
----------------------------------------------------------------------

*Key assign*

x:Light Punch
y:Middle Punch
z:Hard Punch
a:Light Kick
b:Middle Kick
c:Hard Kick
start:Taunt

*System*

Taunt
 -start

*Special Moves*

Hand Squash
 -(near opponent)F+z

Hand 'Shake'
 -(near opponent)B+z

Love Torture
 -a+b/b+c/a+c

*Super Moves*

Fire Ball
 -D,DF,F+x/y/z

Ice Ball
 -D,DB,B+x/y/z

Thunder Ball
 -B,DB,D,DF,F+x/y/z

Axel Spin
 -D,DB,B+a/b/c
 -can also be done in air

Axel Spin(EX)
 -D,DB,B+a/b/c
 -can also be done in air
 -use hero gauge

Kossak Dance
 -D,DF,F+a/b/c

*Hyper Moves*

The Secret Garden
 -D,DB,B,F+a/b/c
 -Use Power level at 1000

The Secret Garden(EX)
 -D,DB,B,F+a/b/c
 -Use Power level at 1000
 -use hero gauge
