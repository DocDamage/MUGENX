CHARACTER PROFILE (taken from Evoga's website)
==============================================================================================

Name:              JAMES LEWIS
Age:               24
Birthday:          OCTOBER 8th
Nationality:       AMERICAN
Height:            6'0''
Weight:            183lbs
Blood type:        A
Fighting style:    RYU ZUI KEN + STREET FIGHT
Likes:             GOOD OPPONENTS
Favorite food:     ITALIAN PASTA, BEER.
Favorite sport:    STREET FIGHTING.
Most value:        OLD COAT
Most hate:         SCAR, LOSE A FIGHT.



Hardboiled, explosive and very unsociable, he escaped from his home along with his brother Billy
and lived on the streets for a while until they were take by an uncle who was a cop in the 
Sunshine city department, he put both brothers under the guidance of master Le Song, while young 
he had always problems with the local gangs of Sunshine city and he was the type of guy that do 
not follow any rules. He was under the guidance of the old master Le Song to learn and train in 
order to take the Red Dragon spirit without knowing the plan of the old master.

When a powerful gang, angered because Jimmy reject them to join the gang attacked Jimmy's 
girlfriend, he was not able to protect her and she died, Jimmy went in search of vengeance 
destroying all the band with the help of his younger brother Billy. He blames himself for the 
death and the relation with his brother broke apart, he left the training and became a lone 
fighter, picking up fights to earn money or just for the thrill of the action that burns inside 
his tormented soul, in those circuits he earned fame of be a tough opponent, and is there an 
unknown fighter hurts him badly leaving a scar in his face.

In these low places he meets Sonia, an assassin for hire that will challenge him, he refuses 
but Sonia insist so after a tight fight where he won Sonia started to follow him from town to 
town close in the shadows.

Jimmy, because of his natural resonance with the Red Dragon spirit knows that something is 
wrong and decide to return to the city he left to forget his past.

Appearances:   Rage of the Dragons

"Other" games: Double Dragon
               Double Dragon II - The Revenge
               Double Dragon III - The Sacred Stones
               Super Double Dragon
               Double Dragon V - The Shadow Falls
               Double Dragon (Neo-Geo version)
               Battletoads/Double Dragon

USAGE
==============================================================================================

1 Get Mugen from Elecbyte at www.elecbyte.com

2 After setting up Mugen, click on the "chars" directory and create a new folder called
"jimmylee". Unzip the files into this directory.

4 Open up SELECT.CFG (found in the "data" directory) and insert the following line
jimmylee, stages/"background".def

..where "background" is the name of the background you wish to use with Jimmy.

5 Run Mugen

6 Enjoy
