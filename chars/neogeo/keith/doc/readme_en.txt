 How to play
----------------------------------------------------------------------

*Key assign*

x:Light Punch
y:Hard Punch
z:
a:Light Kick
b:Hard Kick
c:
start:Taunt

*System*

Air jump
 -(in the air)U/UF/BU

Burst
 -Whenever one gauge accumulates,it moves automatically.

Taunt
 -Start

*Special Moves*

Throw
 -(near opponent)F+y

Air Throw
 -(in the air,near opponent)F+y

Strike attack
 -b+y/c

Counter
 -(during guarding)b+y/c
 -Power level at 1000

Dir+Button Attack1
 -F+a

Dir+Button Attack2
 -F+b

*Super Moves*

Lightning Slash
 -D,DB,B+x/y

Knuckle Bomber
 -B(charge),F+x/y

Rolling Cannon
 -D,DB,B+a/b

Spiral Kick
 -D,DF,F+a/b
 -Can also be done in the air

Stress Shot
 -D,DB,B,DB,D,DF,F+y
 -Power level at 1000

Ippatsu Ougi
 -D,DF,F,D,DF,F+y
 -Power level at 2000
