    At the age of 12 Oni lit fire on the dorm rooms of the orphanage and
  escape along with Cassandra, with a background of violence in Oni and

    Soon Oni was well known in low Sunshine city because of his extreme
  violence and his fame as a ruthless fighter in the illegal circuits, Oni
  does not have relation with nobody except for one girl from low Sunshine
  city.

    After hearing about a person that knows everything Cassandra decide to
  look for this person in an attempt to know about her past and their parents
  because, even when they are as close as brothers, she is not sure about
  this, so Oni decide to go with her to look for the one that calls himself
  "BLACK DRAGON".

                                                        Where is ONI from? ||
      ת  ת  ת ת ת תת תת תתת-תת-תת-ת-ת-ת--ת--ת---=--=--=-=-=-==-==-============


    Oni is a character from the game Rage Of The Dragons from Evoga
  Entertainment and Noise Factory. The game, story wise, is heavily inspired
  in Double Dragon, and is considered by some people as a sequel to the
  Double Dragon game for the Neo-Geo (it is not). RotD tried to bring some
  fresh air to the Neo-Geo fighting world. While mantaining a basic engine
  similar to the KOF series, RotD is structured around two-on-two fights:
  teams are composed of two characters, and each team has to defeat the other
  one's two members to win. But unlike KOF, teams can switch characters any
  time (with some restrictions), and can even perform some super moves
  together. This leads to some interesting strategies. The game also featured
  a combo system that, although somewhat simplistic, allowed some *very* long
  and flashy combos. All in all, RotD wanted to stand out on its own instead
  of been marked like a KOF wannabe, succeeding in doing so.

    RotD didn't come without some flaws, but it is a damn good game overall,
  especially being the first fighting game from Noise Factory (SNK's first
  fighting games weren't that good anyway).

    1. Unzip the file Oni.zip to the mugen/chars/Oni folder.
    2. Edit the file mugen/data/select.cfg and, below the label
       "[Characters]" (you surely will have a large bunch of character
       names here) add:

        kfm, stages/kfm.def            \
        sho, stages/shobg.def          |- Other chars in your select.def 
        daolong, stages/daobg.def      /

        Oni                          <-- Add this line

    That's all!

                                                           Basic Controls ||
      ת  ת  ת ת ת תת תת תתת-תת-תת-ת-ת-ת--ת--ת---=--=--=-=-=-==-==-============


    Well, tap F, F to run forward, B, B to hop back. You already knew that
  :D. There is however an important aspect involving backdashing, and it's
  that while Oni is hopping back he's totally invincible from start to end,
  so use it wisely.

    There are also four types of jump:

    A Normal Jump is just what the name says, a normal jump :).

    Hyper Jumps are jumps with extended horizontal range, you can get to the
  other side of the screen with one of these. Extremely useful to approach an
  enemy from a long distance, and also to make a fast retreat.

    Short Jumps are, again, short jumps :). If you have sometimes played KOF,
  you know the drill: these are really effective to initiate an assault. They
  are also a bit difficult to pull off.

    Hyper Short Jumps are a combination of the above types of jump: low jumps
  with more horizontal range. These are the best for surprise attacks from
  medium distances, and also the hardest to pull. But it pays off in VS
  matches :).



      ת  ת  ת ת ת תת תת תתת-תת-תת-ת-ת-ת--ת--ת---=--=--=-=-=-==-==-============
                                                     Defensive Techniques ||
      ת  ת  ת ת ת תת תת תתת-תת-תת-ת-ת-ת--ת--ת---=--=--=-=-=-==-==-============


** Dodge                                                            LP + LK **

    Oni will lean towards the background for a short time, effectively
  dodging any attack. But there is a catch, your opponent can grab you any
  time during a dodge, so don't overuse it.

    Use Dodges manly to avoid projectiles and against predictable opponents.
  Dodging near the enemy is a risky wage, as you are sitting duck just
  waiting to be thrown.


** Roll Forward/Back                                          F/B + LP + LK **

    Rolls are basically a Dodge while moving. Oni will roll along the ground
  for a relatively short distance avoiding any attack (except throws),
  passing through the enemy. But, unlike Dodges, Rolls have a very brief
  period of vulnerability at the end.

    Rolls are very useful in gaining the upper hand in the fight, allowing
  you to play aggressively. Just don't get too predictable with these
  (against human opponent, of course).


** Counter Attack                     B + SP + SK, while blocking an attack
                                                   ( 1 Power Bar required ) **

    Oni will counter the enemy attack with a fierce blow with his claw,   
  sending the opponent to the other side of the screen if it connects. This
  attack has a long invencibility period at the beginning which lasts until
  the claw is fully extended.

    The Counter Attack becomes very useful when under heavy pressure. Useful
  against moves with high block damage or to get rid the enemy if we get
  trapped in the corner. On the other hand, the damage caused is tiny, as
  this is a pure defensively attack (¨?), and it does cost 1 Power Bar, which
  shouldn't be much of an issue because Oni builds up power quite fast.


** Safe Landing             LP + LK, near to ground after being knocked out **

    Just when he was about to fall on his back, Oni recovers and rolls along
  the floor, away from the enemy.

    Safe Landing is a basic technique when it comes to avoiding damage and
  playing offensively. By Safe Landing you will avoid any on-the-ground
  attack the enemy could try, screw his timing and not allow him to think or
  reposition himself, not to mention avoiding any meaty attack calculated
  to hit just as you get up. Oni is also invincible while rolling, so you
  should try a Safe Landing whenever possible.

                                                     Offensive Techniques ||
      ת  ת  ת ת ת תת תת תתת-תת-תת-ת-ת-ת--ת--ת---=--=--=-=-=-==-==-============


** Throw                              F/B + SP, while close to the opponent **

    Oni will grab his opponent and deliver a strong slash in the face,
  sending the opponent far away. The direction pressed will determine which
  way the enemy will be sent after being slashed. Use this to your advantage,
  sending the opponent to the corner whenever you can for example.


** Air Throw                     F + SP in air, while close to the opponent **

    Oni will grab his opponent, spin with him around a bit, then crush him
  against the ground. It's a good move which gives Oni some supremacy in air,
  although it gets out by chance most of the time.


** First Impact                                                     SP + SK **

    The trademark Rage Of The Dragons attack technique. Oni will slide
  forward with a long range claw attack. If it connects, the opponent will be
  sent slowly flying, and you can start a FI combo. Five letters will appear
  below Oni, meaning a series of buttons (in Neo-Geo notation: A is light
  punch, B is light kick, and so on). Push this buttons in sequence and Oni
  will land one hit after another on the opponent. The last hit will knock
  the opponent away, but the best part is that you can follow it with more
  attacks.

    There are two types of FI combos: Ground and Air FI combos. Hold the Up
  direction after the FI combo starter hits for an Air FI combo, else Oni
  will go for a Ground FI combo. Each one has its own prefixated sequence of
  buttons, but you'll learn them in no time. Although both types deal the
  same damage (not much), they are different. Ground FI combos will build
  less power, but you can combo the last hit with any Special or Super move
  (some will conect only on certain circumstances). Air FI combos build twice
  the power than Ground ones, but you can't only combo a Cutting Edge
  afterwards. You land first though, so you have time to connect a Super
  afterwards. Bear that in mind.

    FI combos are excellent to punish mistakes from the enemy, but you must
  make sure it will connect or you'll suffer. Note that the FI combo starter
  has noticeably lag before and after the slash. Unfortunately, you can't
  combo his FI combo starter from anything.

                                                       Additional Attacks ||
      ת  ת  ת ת ת תת תת תתת-תת-תת-ת-ת-ת--ת--ת---=--=--=-=-=-==-==-============


** Backhand Claw                                                     F + LP **

    Oni steps forward slashing with a turning two-hit claw attack. The first
  hit, which is quite short-ranged, will combo from any Strong attack, while
  the second will only combo (provided the first one missed) from a crouching
  Strong punch or the first hit of a close String kick. It's cancellable on
  both hits. This attack doesn't deal a lot of damage but it's very good for
  extending combos or starting an assault from afar. Just bear in mind that
  the first hit will sometimes miss in combos.


** Vertical Kick                                                    DF + LK **

    Oni will deliver a very high angled (almost painful) upward kick. Oni
  leans back a bit while doing it, so it's horizontal range is very poor,
  though it's more than it seems. Due to it's lack of horizontal range it
  won't combo from anything except the first hit of a standing Strong punch,
  and them it will sometimes miss. It's primary use is as an antiair attack,
  especially for very close ones, with which it works especially well. It's
  cancellable, which adds to its versatility. As you will see as you read,
  Oni is very strong against air attacks.

                                                            Special Moves ||
      ת  ת  ת ת ת תת תת תתת-תת-תת-ת-ת-ת--ת--ת---=--=--=-=-=-==-==-============


** Air Cut                                                     F, D, DF + P **

    Oni unleashes a large downward slash that will shock the opponent with
  electricity if it connects. The Light version is fast and comboes from any
  Strong attack and Light kick attacks. The Strong version won't combo from
  Light attacks and some Strong ones, but delivers two hits for slightly more
  damage.

    This is one of Oni's more versatile moves. It's good for combos, is an
  excellent antiair, has excellent range and knocks the opponent away. It's
  the only special move which comboes from a Light attack. It will cancel
  enemy projectiles. On top of that, if Oni is hit out of it while slashing,
  the slash itself will hit the enemy. It's only downside is it has a long
  recovery time, so beware.


** Cutting Edge                                             D, B + P in air **

    Oni stops in mid-air and unleashes a large downward slash that will shock
  the opponent with electricity if it connects. The Light version is fast and
  hits once. The Strong version takes more time to execute but delivers two
  hits for slightly more damage. Both versions will combo from any jumping
  Strong attack.

    This move is key to establish Oni's supremacy in the air. It has extreme
  range, plus all the other advantages of the Air Cut (knock away, cancels
  projectiles, priority). Of course, if you miss you're screwed as you need
  to land to recover control.


** The Ripper                                                   B, D, F + P **

    Oni stops for a moment, then dashes forward a short distance, trying to
  grab the opponent. If he makes it, will lift the enemy for a second, then
  rip them apart, sending the enemy far away. The Strong version will slide
  twice the distance than the Light one.

    This is one of those psychological attacks. It's very slow to come out,
  so it won't combo from anything, period. But it has a catch: the grab is
  unblockable. Most human opponents will try to block it and then counter,
  which will prove painful for them :). It's more risky to use against the
  computer, especially against AI's, as thay usually aren't that naive. Don't
  get predictable with this as it can be avoided with a simple jump, just use
  this move from time to time to confuse the enemy :).


** Cold Steel                                D, B + P, repeat up to 3 times **

    Oni slides forward slashing once, twice, and finishing with a donward
  slash which will knock the opponent away. The Light version will slide a
  short distance before the first slash and comboes from any Strong attack,
  while the Strong version will slide further but will only combo from a
  Backhand Slash or the first hit of a standing Strong kick. Both cause the
  same damage.

    This is Oni's trademark attack (although certainly not original), and one
  you will be usinig a lot. It does very good damage, the Light version is
  fast and very comboable, and you can play mind games with it. If blocked,
  don't do the three slashes. Stop after the second one, which is the fastest
  to recover. You can also delay the second slash, which comes out very fast,
  hitting the enemy if he tries to punish you after blocking the first one.



      ת  ת  ת ת ת תת תת תתת-תת-תת-ת-ת-ת--ת--ת---=--=--=-=-=-==-==-============
                                                              Super Moves ||
      ת  ת  ת ת ת תת תת תתת-תת-תת-ת-ת-ת--ת--ת---=--=--=-=-=-==-==-============


** Bloody Mess                                               D, F, D, F + K **

    Oni prepares himself and then unleash a *huge* upward slash. The Light
  version hits once and need 1 Power Bar. The Strong version consumes 2 Power
  Bars, hits up to three times, and has an additional dashing claw attack at
  the end, which can be delayed by holding the SK button.

    The Bloody Mess is the most versatile move at Oni's disposal. It works
  wonderfully as an antiair. It works wonderfully in combos. It has extreme
  range, and is rather safe. It's wonderful overall :). The extra claw attack
  in the Strong version is also a very nice bonus. Many people will try to
  punish you if you miss the initial slash attack, and won't be ready for it.
  It deals more damage than the slash attack, so make sure to connect with
  it. It can be delayed for quite a long time, enough for the opponent to
  land and get up if you want. If the opponent blocks it, he will get guard
  crushed (dealing only block damage).

First Impact (FI) combos:
-------------------------

Ground - A, A, C, C, D  (LP, LP, SP, SP, SK)
Air    - A, B, C, C, D  (LP, LK, SP, SP, SK)


-Jump SK, FI, LP, LP, SP, SP, SK, Air Cut

-Jump SK, FI, LP, LP, SP, SP, SK, Cold Steel x 3

-Jump SK, FI, hold Up, LP, LK, SP, SP, SK, Cutting Edge

-Jump SK, FI, hold Up, LP, LK, SP, SP, SK, Bloody Mess


Other combos:
-------------

-Jump SK, close SP(2 hits), Backhand Claw (2 hits), Cold Steel x 3, Air Cut
                                                             or Bloody Mess

    In order to connect both hits of the close SP, you will likely need a
  Hyper Jump or a cornered opponent.

-Jump SK, close SK(1 hits), Backhand Claw (1 hit), Cold Steel x 3, Air Cut
                                                             or Bloody Mess

    Easier to connect, but less hits.

-Close SP(1 hit), Vertical Kick, Bloody Mess

-Jump SK, (land and jump again), Jump SK, Cutting Edge
