  Controls
  --------

    Basic Controls
    --------------
      D     - down
      F     - forward
      U     - up
      B     - back
      x     - weak punch
      y     - strong punch
      z     - Especial
      a     - weak kick
      b     - strong kick
      c     - Especial
      start - Taunt

      
    Basic Movements
    -----------------
      F,F                       - Dash
      F,F, Hold F               - Run
      B,B                       - Back Step
      x+a                       - Tactical Step (uses 0.5 power bar)
      F,F, during guard         - Guard Cancel Tactical Step (uses 1 power bar)
      x+a, during normal attack - AC Tactical Step (uses 1 power bar)
      c, during guard           - Guard Cancel (uses 1 power bar)
      x+a, when knocked down    - Fall Breaker/Safe Fall
      y+b                       - Top Hunting Throw

    Normal Attacks
    ---------------
      Robo Arm: DF + x
      Whirlwind Fist: B or F + y
      Hyper Tackle: DB or DF + b
      
    
    Special Attacks
    ---------------
      Joy Joy Block: D, DF, F + x or y or xy (or z)
      Baseball Stars: D, DB, B + x
      Baseball Stars Professional: D, DB, B + y
      Baseball Stars 2: D, DB, B + xy (or z)
      Bushinken: D, DF, F + a or b
      2020 Super Catch: D, DB, B + a or b
      Joy Joy Balloon: axy (or c)
        > Joy Joy Treasure: x or y
        > Joy Joy Kick: a
        > Joy Joy Block: D, DF, F + x or y
        > Lighting Ball: axy (or c)
        > Cancel: b
          >> Coma: x or y
          >> Moonsault: a or b
            
     
    Hyper Attacks
    -------------
      Joy Joy Party: D, DF, F, D, DF, F + x or y
      Last Resort: F, DF, D, DB, B, F, DF, D, DB, B, x or y
        > Power! Unit!: D, DB, B + x or y

    
    Combo Notes
    ------------

      1. Jump with any attack -> Strong Punch (1/2 hit) -> Whirlwind Fist

      2. Crouch Light Punch -> Crouch Light Kick -> Hyper Tackle -> Baseball Stars or Joy Joy Party

      3. Crouch Light Punch -> Crouch Light Kick -> Hyper Tackle -> Baseball Stars -> SC with Joy Joy Party

      4.  Jump with any attack -> Strong Punch (1 hit) -> Robo Arm -> Hyper Tackle -> Baseball Stars -> SC with Joy Joy Party

      5. Last Resort -> Bushinken (strong version) -> Light Punch -> Robo Arm -> Hyper Tackle -> Baseball Stars -> SC with Joy Joy Party (this is very dificult combo, hehe)

      6. Joy Joy Treasure -> Lighting Ball (or Joy Joy Kick (x5))
      


    Notes
    ------------ 

      1. Super Cancel no needs power bar stocks;
      
      2. Bushinken, Baseball Stars and Baseball Stars Professional are super cancelable;

      3. Baseball Stars and Baseball Stars Professional can refflect projectiles;

      4. 2020 Super Catch convert projectiles (add 0.250 on power bar);

      5. Joy Joy Block (balloon version) forces Ai to leave Joy Joy Balloon;

      6. For make the Joy Joy Blocks faster push down during the bginning of Joy Joy Block motion.
