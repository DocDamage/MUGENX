 How to play
----------------------------------------------------------------------

*Key assign*

x:Light Punch
y:Hard Punch
z:No use
a:Light Kick
b:Hard Kick
c:Tactical Step/Gurad Cancel Attack
start:Taunt

*System*

Taunt
-start

Tactical Step
-x+a/c
-Power level at 500

Gurad Cancel Step
-(While guarding)F,F
-Power level at 500

Gurad Cancel Attack
-(While guarding)y+b/c
-Power level at 1000

*Special Moves*

Swearing Smash
-(near opponent)y+b

Round Heel Kick
-F+b

Snappig Stamp Kick
-B+b

*Super Moves*

Hearty Brandy Shoe Type A
-D,DB,B+x
-Can also be done during Hearty Brandy Shoe Type B

Hearty Brandy Shoe Type B
-D,DB,B+a
-Can also be done during Hearty Brandy Shoe Type C

Hearty Brandy Shoe Type C
-D,DB,B+y
-Can also be done during Hearty Brandy Shoe Type D

Hearty Brandy Shoe Type D
-D,DB,B+b

Hearty Brandy Shoe Type F
-(during Hearty Brandy Shoe Type A)D,DF,F+a

Hip Bazooka
-D,DF,F+x/y

Rhythmic Bound
-(during Hip Bazooka)x/y/a/b

Rhythmic Kick
-(during Rhythmic Bound)x/y/a/b

Cutey Rainbow
-F,D,DF+a/b

Happy Presser
-(during Cutey Rainbow hitting on the ground)a/b

Cheerleader Kick
-D,DF,F+a/b

Vaulting Over
-(near opponent)F,DF,D,DB,B+x/y

*Hyper Moves*

Splash Air Dance
 -D,DF,F,D,DF,F+a/b
 -Power level at 1000
 -Can also be done in the air

Jealousy Bomber
 -(near opponent)B,DB,D,DF,F,B,DB,D,DF,F+x/y
 -Power level at 3000
