 How to play
----------------------------------------------------------------------

*Key assign*

x:Light Punch
y:Hard Punch
z:No use
a:Light Kick
b:Hard Kick
c:Tactical Step/Gurad Cancel Attack
start:Taunt

*System*

Taunt
-start

Tactical Step
-x+a/c
-Power level at 500

Gurad Cancel Step
-(While guarding)F,F
-Power level at 500

Gurad Cancel Attack
-(While guarding)c
-Power level at 1000

*Special Moves*

Ura Nage
 -(near opponent)y+b

Shoteida
 -F+y

Izuna Kiri
 -(in the air)D+x

*Super Moves*

Rekkozan
 -D,DF,F+x/y

Double Rekkozan
 -D,DF,F,D,DF,F+x/y
 -Power level at 500

Kohryuha
 -F,D,DF+x/y

Ninpoh Kohrin Kazan
 -D,DB,B+x/y/a/b

Ninja Leg Lariat
 -B,D,DB+a/b

Mijin Kakure no Jutsu
 -D,U+x/y/a/b
 -Can also be done in the air

*Hyper Moves*

Iga Ninpo Ougi Hachimon Danko Zan
 -D,DF,F,D,DF,F+a/b
 -Power level at 1000

Iga Ninpo Ougi Bonten Senkoh Jin
 -D,DB,B,D,DB,B+a/b
 -Power level at 2000
 -Can also be done in the air
