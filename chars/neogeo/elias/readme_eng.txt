                                                             Who is ELIAS? ||
      ת  ת  ת ת ת תת תת תתת-תת-תת-ת-ת-ת--ת--ת---=--=--=-=-=-==-==-============


    Elias is a member of a high class family, who eventually became a priest.
  Learned about his exorcism talent in jail, accused of a murder he didn't
  commit. It was during his stay in prison where he got as buffed up as he is
  now. When he got out from jail, he became interested in putting his
  exorcising abilities to good use and expel evil spirits from people. His
  first objectives were two young Japanese brothers called Oni and Cassandra,
  he did all that he could but they ran away from the shelter for some
  obscure reasons. After that, he was required to look upon a little English
  girl called Alice Carroll, who was possessed by some evil spirit. She was
  transferred to America as her condition went worse, and eventually escaped.
  Completely determined to accomplish his task this time, he sets out to
  find the little girl and help her.

                                                      Where is ELIAS from? ||
      ת  ת  ת ת ת תת תת תתת-תת-תת-ת-ת-ת--ת--ת---=--=--=-=-=-==-==-============


    Elias Patrick is a character from the game Rage Of The Dragons from Evoga
  Entertainment and Noise Factory. The game, story wise, is heavily inspired
  in Double Dragon, and is considered by some people as a sequel to the
  Double Dragon game for the Neo-Geo (it is not). ROTD tried to bring some
  fresh air to the Neo-Geo fighting world. While mantaining a basic engine
  similar to the KOF series, ROTD is structured around two-on-two fights:
  teams are composed of two characters, and each team has to defeat the other
  one's two members to win. But unlike KOF, teams can switch characters any
  time (with some restrictions), and can even perform some super moves
  together. This leads to some interesting strategies. The game also featured
  a combo system that, although somewhat simplistic, allowed some *very* long
  and flashy combos. All in all, ROTD wanted to stand out on its own instead
  of been marked like a KOF wannabe, succeeding in doing so.

    ROTD didn't come without some flaws, but it is a damn good game overall,
  especially being the first fighting game from Noise Factory (SNK's first
  fighting games weren't that good anyway).

    1. Unzip the file Elias.zip to the mugen/chars/Elias folder.

        kfm, stages/kfm.def            \

        daolong, stages/daobg.def      /

        Elias                          <-- Add this line

    That's all!

                                                           Basic Controls ||
      ת  ת  ת ת ת תת תת תתת-תת-תת-ת-ת-ת--ת--ת---=--=--=-=-=-==-==-============


    Well, tap F, F to run forward, B, B to hop back. You already knew that
  :D. There is however an important aspect involving backdashing, and it's
  that while Elias is hopping back he's totally invincible from start to end,
  so use it wisely.

    There are also four types of jump:

    A Normal Jump is just what the name says, a normal jump :).

    Hyper Jumps are jumps with extended horizontal range, you can get to the
  other side of the screen with one of these. Extremely useful to approach an
  enemy from a long distance, and also to make a fast retreat.

    Short Jumps are, again, short jumps :). If you have sometimes played KOF,
  you know the drill: these are really effective to initiate an assault. They
  are also a bit difficult to pull off.

    Hyper Short Jumps are a combination of the above types of jump: low jumps
  with more horizontal range. These are the best for surprise attacks from
  medium distances, and also the hardest to pull. But it pays off in VS
  matches :).



      ת  ת  ת ת ת תת תת תתת-תת-תת-ת-ת-ת--ת--ת---=--=--=-=-=-==-==-============
                                                     Defensive Techniques ||
      ת  ת  ת ת ת תת תת תתת-תת-תת-ת-ת-ת--ת--ת---=--=--=-=-=-==-==-============


** Dodge                                                            LP + LK **

    Elias will lean towards the background for a short time, effectively
  dodging any attack. But there is a catch, your opponent can grab you any
  time during a dodge, so don't overuse it.

    Use Dodges manly to avoid projectiles and against predictable opponents.
  Dodging near the enemy is a risky wage, as you are sitting duck just
  waiting to be thrown.


** Roll Forward/Back                                          F/B + LP + LK **

    Rolls are basically a Dodge while moving. Elias will roll along the
  ground for a relatively short distance avoiding any attack (except throws),
  passing through the enemy. But, unlike Dodges, Rolls have a very brief
  period of vulnerability at the end.

    Rolls are very useful in gaining the upper hand in the fight, allowing
  you to play aggressively. Just don't get too predictable with these
  (against human opponent, of course).


** Counter Attack                     B + SP + SK, while blocking an attack
                                                   ( 1 Power Bar required ) **

    Elias will counter the enemy attack with two fierce punches, sending the
  opponent to the other side of the screen if the second one connects. The
  first punch doesn't have much range but is invincible, while the second
  has long reach but can be hit out of it.

    The Counter Attack becomes very useful when under heavy pressure. Useful
  against moves with high block damage or to get rid the enemy if we get
  trapped in the corner. On the other hand, the damage caused is tiny, as
  this is a pure defensively attack (¨?), and it does cost 1 Power Bar, which
  shouldn't be much of an issue because Elias builds up power quite fast.



      ת  ת  ת ת ת תת תת תתת-תת-תת-ת-ת-ת--ת--ת---=--=--=-=-=-==-==-============
                                                     Offensive Techniques ||
      ת  ת  ת ת ת תת תת תתת-תת-תת-ת-ת-ת--ת--ת---=--=--=-=-=-==-==-============


** Throw                              F/B + SP, while close to the opponent **

    Elias will grab his opponent and deliver a strong punch to the face,
  sending the opponent far away. The direction pressed will determine which
  way the enemy will be sent after being punched. Use this to your advantage,
  sending the opponent to the corner whenever you can for example.


** First Impact                                                     SP + SK **

    The trademark Rage Of The Dragons attack technique. Elias will slide
  forward with an upward punch. If it connects, the opponent will be sent
  slowly flying, and you can start a FI combo. Five letters will appear below
  Elias, meaning a series of buttons (in Neo-Geo notation, A is light punch,
  B is light kick, and so on). Push this buttons in sequence and Elias will
  land one hit after another on the opponent. The last hit will knock the
  opponent away, but the best part is that you can follow it with more
  attacks.

    There are two types of FI combos: Ground and Air FI combos. Hold the Up
  direction after the FI combo starter hits for an Air FI combo, else Elias
  will go for a Ground FI combo. Each one has its own prefixated sequence of
  buttons, but you'll learn them in no time. Although both types deal the
  same damage (not much), they are different. Ground FI combos will build
  less power, but you can combo the last hit with any Special or Super move
  (some will conect only on certain circumstances, or won't connect at all).
  Air FI combos build twice the power than Ground ones, but the last hit
  isn't bufferable (not for Elias). You land first though, so you have time
  to connect a Super afterwards. Bear that in mind.

    FI combos are excellent to punish mistakes from the enemy, but you must
  make sure it will connect or you'll suffer. Note that the FI combo starter
  doesn't have that much range (although it has more than it looks like), and
  has noticeably lag before and after the punch. You can't combo the FI combo
  starter from anything except from a Strong jumping attack, but you must hit
  *very* deep for that.



      ת  ת  ת ת ת תת תת תתת-תת-תת-ת-ת-ת--ת--ת---=--=--=-=-=-==-==-============
                                                       Additional Attacks ||
      ת  ת  ת ת ת תת תת תתת-תת-תת-ת-ת-ת--ת--ת---=--=--=-=-=-==-==-============


** Overhead Punch                                                    F + LP **

    Elias will take some impulse and then deliver a strong downwards punch.
  This move is obviously an overhead, and must be blocked high. It is too
  slow to be comboed from anything, but the scary thing is it's cancellable,
  so you can combo any Special or Super move after connecting. Risky to use
  against the computer, but very useful to fool human players and break their
  defense, especially if mixed up with his other Additional Attack.


** Double Low Kick                                                   F + LK **

    Elias will slide forward a bit and then hit with two low kicks which must
  be blocked low. Although it has some startup lag, it's fast enough to be
  comboed from any Strong attack. On top of that, it's cancellable on both
  hits, giving you a lot of time to input any move. You will be using this a
  lot to extend your combos. It's a bit dangerous to use this on its own
  though, due to the heavy lag after the second kick.



      ת  ת  ת ת ת תת תת תתת-תת-תת-ת-ת-ת--ת--ת---=--=--=-=-=-==-==-============
                                                            Special Moves ||
      ת  ת  ת ת ת תת תת תתת-תת-תת-ת-ת-ת--ת--ת---=--=--=-=-=-==-==-============


** Sinful                         F, D, DF + P, while close to the opponent **

    Elias will try to grab his opponent. If he makes it, he will punish the
  opposition with a series of light-speed punches. If he doesn't make it, he
  will stand there for a second, looking like an idiot, very vulnerable. The
  good thing is that it can be done in a combo, and that it's unblockable.
  It's also the only special move which will combo from Light attacks, though
  it's difficult to do so. The bad thing is it has poor range, though more
  than a throw. It's a good surprise attack overall, but as always, don't
  overuse it. It's risky to do in a combo because it can miss, and besides
  Elias has better options when it comes to combos.


** Magnus                                                          D, B + P **

    Elias raises his arm and summons a burst of sacred fire in front of him,
  burning and knocking down the opponent. The Light version is stationary,
  and fast enough to be comboed from any Strong attack. The Strong version
  will move Elias some distance forward, but is slower and can only be
  comboed from the first hit of a Double Low Kick. Both versions deal the
  same damage, and will cancel projectiles.

    This is Elias' most versatile Special move. It's excellent to end combos
  with, it knocks the oponent down and provides some decent cover behind
  him. The projectile cancelling is a nice bonus, though it's usually best to
  use a Dodge or Roll. Another positive point is that if Elias is hit out of
  it, the flame will still nail the enemy, so it's rather safe. Don't rely
  too much on it as an antiair though, as it vertical range is shorter than
  it looks.


** Hell or Heaven                                               F, D, B + K **

    Elias will stop for a second, then run forward trying to grab his
  opponent. If he succeeds, will lift the opponent and burn him with holy
  fire, draining some life from him. 40% of the damage caused to the enemy
  will be transferred to Elias. The Light version makes Elias run about half
  a screen lenght, while the Strong version will cover an entire screen. Due
  to the initial pause, it will only combo from an Overhead Punch, or the
  first hit of: a standing close Strong punch, a crouching Strong punch, or a
  Double Low Kick.

    This move is key to survival, thanks to its revitalizing properties of
  course. It's deadly in combos, but difficult to connect on its own, so use
  with caution.


** Holy Flare                                                      D, F + P **

    Elias will raise a hand and summon a large ring of holy fire, then will
  toss it to his opponent. The Light version will release a medium speed
  fireball, and the Strong version a faster one. Damage is the same in both.
  This move is too slow to be comboed from anything.

    At first sight, this move seems of little practical use, due to the time
  it takes before actually releasing the fireball. That's right, you aren't
  winning any fireball fights with this. So what's this move useful for?
  For pressure and to keep the opponent at bay. Unlike many other fireballs,
  the Holy Flare is rather big and is launched quite high, so, unless the
  opponent has a very high jump, or can double or triangle jump, will be
  forced to block, giving you time to approach (or throw more fireballs if
  you're feeling especially cheap). Another interesting feature of the Holy
  Flare is that, although it doesn't knock down, the enemy is stunned for
  quite a long time. This isn't advantageous up close, but if you manage to
  nail the enemy with a Holy Flare from a distance, the stun period is long
  enough to allow some other more damaging move to combo, namely...

                                                              Super Moves ||
      ת  ת  ת ת ת תת תת תתת-תת-תת-ת-ת-ת--ת--ת---=--=--=-=-=-==-==-============


** Maximum Desiderium                                        F, D, B, F + P **

    Elias concentrates, then sprints forward at high speed, trying to grab
  the opponent. If he connects, will lift the opponent and burn him with holy
  flames first with one hand, then with both, sucking the life off him/her,
  throwing him/her away afterwards. 40% of the damage caused to the enemy
  will be transferred to Elias. The Light version will launch a Level 1
  Super, consuming 1 Power Bar, while the Strong one will launch a Level 2
  Super, consuming 2 Power Bars, causing mure damage (and regaining more life
  back too). This move *can't* be air blocked.

    The Maximum Desiderium is similar in concept to the Hell or Heaven, but
  there are some very important differencies (besides damage). Unlike the
  HoH, The MD has zero startup lag and is very fast. This makes the MD a
  highly versatile move, as it's very easy to punish enemy mistakes with
  this. This also means you can combo it from pretty much any cancellable
  attack. And another consequence is that you can use the Holy Flare as a
  nasty setup for the MD: launch a HF from a distance and, if the opponent
  gets hit or blocks it in the air, initiate a MD, and the enemy won't be
  able of doing anything but eat it :).

First Impact (FI) combos:
-------------------------

Ground - A, B, A, C, D  (LP, LK, LP, SP, SK)
Air    - A, C, D, D, D  (LP, SP, SK, SK, SK)


-Jump SK, FI, LP, LK, LP, SP, SK, Magnus

-Jump SK, FI, LP, LK, LP, SP, SK, Hell or Heaven

-Jump SK, FI, LP, LK, LP, SP, SK, Maximum Desiderium

     This combo depends on the situation and opponent: depending on the
  enemy (more specifically, how his collision boxes are set up), the MD may
  connect immediately or may whiff, causing Elias to pass below the opponent.
  However, if the enemy is at 1/3 screen distance or less from the corner
  when the final hit of the FI connects, the MD will nail the opponent on the
  way down even if it didn't hit at first.

-Jump SK, FI, hold Up, LP, SP, SK, SK, SK, Maximum Desiderium


Other combos:
-------------

-Jump SK, close SP(2 hits), Double Low Kick (2 hits), Light Magnus
-Jump SK, close SP(2 hits), Double Low Kick (1 hit), Hell or Heaven
-Jump SK, close SP(1 hit), Double Low Kick (1 hit), Sinful
-Jump SK, close SP(2 hits), Double Low Kick (1 hit), Maximum Desiderium


Trick Combos:
-------------

-Close LK, Overhead Punch, Holy Flare (his close LK must be blocked low. ^_^)
-Close SP(1 hit), Double Low Kick or Overhead Punch, Light Magnus.
   Mix up his Double Low Kick and Overhead Punch.
-Light Holy Flare, opponent air blocks it, Maximum Desiderium. At the right
 distance, Elias can catch the opponent with his super before he lands from
 the air block.  ^_^
