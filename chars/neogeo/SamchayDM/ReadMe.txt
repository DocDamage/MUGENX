----------------------
I. Features: (v0.8a)
----------------------

- All the essential stuff;
- 1 intro;
- 2 winposes;
- 2 Throws;
- 4 special moves;
- 3 super moves;
- Effects from various games;
- Combo system (unfinished yet);
- Guard counter;
- Forward roll;
- Dodge;
- Charge Power;
- 8 palletes.

III. Movelist:
----------------------

Super Jump       : D, U or D, UF or D, UB
Short Jump       : U(tap)
Guard Counter    : x/y, during guard (stand only)
Forward Roll     : fwd + x + y
Dodge            : x+y
Charge Power     : a+b
???              : F + y
???              : F + b
Flying Fist      : D,DF,F + x/y
Tiger Knee       : D,DF,F + a/b
Shouryuu Knee    : F,DB,D,DF + a/b or F,D,DF + a/b
Knee + elbow     : a + x or b + y
Chou Flying Fist : D,DF,F,D,DF,F + x/y (needs 1 power bar stock)
"Espancamento"   : D,DF,F,D,DF,F + a/b (needs 2 power bar stocks)
Super Grab       : D,DB,B,D,DB,B + x/y (needs 2 power bar stocks)
