-----------------------------------------------------------------------={ INSTALLATION }=--

1/ Open up select.def which is in the data folder

2a/ Type "yagumo" (without the ") under the [Characters] title

2b/ If you have yagumobg.def in the stages folder then type "yagumo, stages/yagumobg.def"

3/ Save and run Mugen

It's as easy as that :D

------------------------------------------------------------------------------={ MOVES }=--

«« Basic Moves

     » Punch  -  X, Y or Z  (Either Standing, Crouching or in Air)

     » Kick  -  X, Y or Z  (Either Standing, Crouching or in Air)

     » Throw - Z or C (Determine the direction you want to throw P2 with F and B)

«« Special Moves:

     » Weapon  -  X+Y  (Either Standing, Crouching or in Air)
         Standing: Yagumo super charges her fan to deal a CRUSHING blow to the enemy!
         Crouching: Yagumo kicks up and hits the enemy twice
         Air: Yagumo spins a fan down to lay the smack on the enemy

     » OhRouZan  -  ~D, F, X or Y or Z
         Yagumo lunges forward with fans to hit the enemy

     » RougaTenRin  -  ~F, D, F, X or Y or Z
         Yagumo spins her fans around to hit the enemy

     » HyakkaRyohRan  -  ~F, D, F, C
         Yagumo deals 3 kicks to the enemy with the last one smacking the enemy away

     » RyuSeiKyaku  -  /$D, C (In Air)
         Yagumo spins around then flys straight down to smack the enemy down

«« Juutsu (Super) Moves:

     » Shirako (A Blue Tiger)  -  ~D, DF, F, D, DF, F, X
         A Blue Tiger comes out from behind Yagumo and leaps at the enemy

     » Narukami (An Orange Dragon)  -  ~D, DF, F, D, DF, F, Y
         A Dragon comes out from behind Yagumo and shoots some flamy gas things at the
         enemy

     » Fudoh (A Fat Swordsman)  -  ~D, DF, F, D, DF, F, Z
         A big fat guy appears to deal a big smack on the enemy
