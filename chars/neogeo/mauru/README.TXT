This is MAURU, a character for MUGEN, the supreme fighting engine.

In order to use him, just place all the files in the .zip in the
directory "chars/mauru" of your mugen directory.

I recommend turning vsync on if you can afford it.

-|                        M O V E S   C H A R T                       |- 
 ---------------------------------------------------------------------- 

The button layout is as follows:

X - Weak punch
Y - Strong punch
A - Weak kick
B - Strong kick

As always, we assume Mauru is facing right.


** Wake-Up Moves **

When you are on the floor, there is a number of things you can do
before getting up:

- Press Forward to roll forward

- Press Back to roll backward

- Press Up to perform a wake-up attack


** Miscellaneous Moves **

Poi:
When close, Forward + Y

Kuuchuu Poi:
When close in air, Forward + Y

Heaven's Fist:
Jump straight Up + Y

WakuWaku Upper:
Right + Y

Double Claw:
Any punch + Any kick

Mauru Slide:        <------ * New *
Crouch-Forward + Y


** Special Moves **
 
Guruguru Punch:
Down, Down-Fwd, Forward + X/Y

Body Press:
Down, Down-Back, Back + A/B

BunBun Copter:
Forward, Down, Down-Fwd + X/Y

Mauru Binta:
(close) Forward, Down-Fwd, Down, Down-Back, Back + B


** WakuWaku Strikers - 1/4 Power Bar required **  <----- * New *

WakuWaku Striker - Bonus Kun:
Back, Down, Down-Back, X

WakuWaku Striker - Slash:
Back, Down, Down-Back, Y

WakuWaku Striker - Tesse:
Back, Down, Down-Back, A

WakuWaku Striker - Dandy-J:
Back, Down, Down-Back, B


** SUPER Mode - 1 Power Bar required **        <-- * New *

SUPER Mode:
A+B+X+Y or Z


** ES(Enhanced Special) Moves - 1 Power Bar required **

ES Guruguru Punch:
Down, Down-Fwd, Forward + X+Y

ES Body Press:
Down, Down-Back, Back + A+B

ES BunBun Copter:
Forward, Down, Down-Fwd + X+Y


** DokiDoki Attack - 1 Power Bar required **

BunBun Typhoon:
Rotate 360§ + X+Y


** HaraHara Attack - 2 Power Bars required **

Miracle Voice:
Down, Down + X+Y/A+B


** Notes **

- Double claw and Body Press are overheads

- You have control of Mauru while performing BunBun Copter

- Miracle Voice is unblockable

- SUPER mode can be used as a counter - You are invincible during the
  explosion
