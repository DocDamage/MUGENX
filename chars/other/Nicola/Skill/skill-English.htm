PROFILE

 

NAME: NICOLA ZA ZA

WEAPON: DISCUS OF DEATH

ORIGINAL GAME: SAVAGE REIGN/ FU'UN MOUKUSHIROKU

 

        Nicola searches to free himself of the Za Za name, made famous by his father, Yah Za Za, captain of industry and the founder of the eponymous Za Za Company. After he attained his doctorate degree at the age of 10, he worked on new forms of body armor to support his weak body. Nicola, now donning his breakthrough "Protector Suit", looks to confront and defeat king lion and take control of the world.
