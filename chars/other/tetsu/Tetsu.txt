Story________________________________________________
(Thanks to ShoShingo)
Yatogi Tetsu. A man with a torn past, preparing to face a cold, uncertain future. Tetsu, the son of a African-American street fighter from Brooklyn and a japanese businesswoman. Raised in the US until the age of 5, his family moved to Japan. His father was part of a syndicate, a powerful organization clouded in mystery, but known for producing effective assasins. After leaving the group, he took his family and fled to Japan for safety. In the years to follow, he would teach Tetsu everything he could about fighting and respect for his life and the life of others. On the day of his tenth birthday, Tetsu woke up to find both of his parents brutally murdered, their bodies beaten almost beyond recognition.

Since Tetsu was not a pure Japanese native, he was ostracized by everyone he went to for help. Cold, alone, and very pissed, he traveled through Japan, searching for anyone who might take him in. One day, a strange old man confroted him. The old man knew of Tetsu's father and offered to train him to gain revenge on those who had killed his parents. Tetsu felt as though he finally had some sort of family again. Until...

On his 20th Birthday, Tetsu once again awoke to find the bloody, disfigured corpse of someone he cared for. The old man, who had taken him in and cared for him like a son, was killed in the exact same way Tetsu's parents were. Alone again, Tetsu decides to head back to America, in hopes of finding some answer as to who, or what, killed every person he has ever cared for. Unknown to him, the enemy is much closer than he could possibly imagine

Upon arrival to America, Tetsu hears of a tournament being held by the syndicate known as Scion, the same syndicate his father was part of. When he arrives, he is greeted, and stalked, by a strange woman. What does this strange woman know about the syndicate? Is she the one who has killed family? To unravel the mystery behind his father's past with the syndicate and to find the answers to the many questions he has, Tetsu will have to fight.

Gameplay Overview__________________________________________________________
Tetsu relies on his speed and potent combos to win. 

Moves Key_________________________________________________________________

F=forward
B=back 
U=up
D=Down
x=weak punck
y=medium punch
z=strong punch
a=weak kick
b=medium kick
c=strong kick

Normal Moves_______________________________________________________________

Dash - Double tap F
-Advance quickly

Hop Back - Double tap B
-Evasive manuever

Chain combo - experiment! 
- Weak-medium-strong is the key. You can even combo in air

Axe kick - F+b
-An alternate medium kick. Cannot be blocked low.

Tetsu Bomber - F or B+y/z
-Can be done in air

Electrocution - F or B+b/c
-Tetsu grabs you, Delivers a few gut punches, and sends you flying into a wall. Acts as a wire attack!

Parry - Just before getting hit-F for high attacks , D for Low
-Deflect your opponents' move or projectile and counter attack!

Fall Parry - D the instant you hit the ground from a fall
-It's hard to do, but worth mastering!

Emergency Recover - QCF+PP while falling and unable to recover (Must be at level 3) 
-Handy for stopping juggles, but uses your entire power bar.... Use with discretion.

Razz - Start
-Tetsu will gain some power when taunting. 

Special Moves__________________________________________________________

Moves with a "*" Can be done as an EX attack using two punches or kicks(Need 1/2 level to use)

*Tetsu Spark  - D,DF,F,punch
-Your basic electric-charged fireball. The EX Version is larger and hits four times.

*Air Tetsu Spark - D,DF,F,punch
-The button you press determines your flight pattern. The EX Version throws three fireballs.

*Tetsu Upper - F,D,DF,punch
-Every Shoto needs a dragon punch. Multi-hit. The EX version acts like a ShinShoryuken. 

*Tetsu Tornado - D,DB,B,kick
-EX Version acts like Messatsu Go Rassen and is a Wire Attack.

Aerial Tetsu Tornado - D,DB,B,kick in air
-The button you press determines it's flight pattern. Great for Aerial Combos and fakes.

*Dive Kick- D,DF,F, kick in air
-WK version is multihit, MK is like Kim's Hishokyaku from KOF'94/'02, WK will pop-up grounded opponents. EX version is a pumped up version of the Weak and Strong Dives.

Assassin's Streak - F,D,DF,kick
-Can go through projectiles and normal attacks. Fast as hell!

*Tetsu Rave  - D,DB,B,punch
-Tetsu does an elbow attack. If it connects, an auto-combo will follow. EX version is more brutal.

Assasin's Slide - D,DF,F,w.kick/m.kick
-Goes under most fireballs. 

Power Kick - D,DF,F,s.kick
-Hold Strong kick to power up. Level 1(not flashing) will knock opponent into the air. Level 2(flashing blue) is a Wire Attack. Level 3(flashing red) is unblockable and is very damaging.

Super Moves (You need one level to use)---------------

Tetsu Blast - D,DF,F,D,DF,F,punch

Tetsu Storm - D,DF,F,D,DF,F,kick

Fighting tips_______________________________________________________________
As already stated, the chaining order is Weak-Medium-Strong. Note this chain is also appicable in the air. 

Specials, EXSpecials, and Supers can be two-in-one from any normal technique. 

You can Super Cancel any Special, EXSpecial, or any Super (must be different from the one you're using) if the move connects. 

There are certain moves called Pop-ups that will launch an opponent into the air, allowing for an aerial rave. Some are obvious (Power Kick, Storm Rave), and some take some cunning to use (Crouch S.Punch, the opponent must be airborne.)

Need a quick escape? Keep in mind that you can two-in-one any special from any normal attack.... including the Silent Streak. But be sure to keep in mind the limitations of the Streak. You can run through the fireball, but not the Shoryuken!!

Know when to block and when to parry. If the move is a single hit and able to be countered, parry it. If the move is multi-hit and you don't have the skill to parry every single hit, or if your opponent's move has an invincibility frame that makes it hard to counter, you're better off blocking. If you're extremely low on health though, try to parry as much as you can. You don't take any block damage if you parry.

If you have enough juggle points, the Assassin's Slide and Kuuchuu Soul Blast can hit grounded opponents.
