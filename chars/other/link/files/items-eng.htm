~ THE ITEMS ~

During the game, besides the rupees, you can collect some items by hitting your opponent (for that, the 3rd option of the customization must be activated). Depending on the customization's options, you can have from 2 to 4 different items. Like the rupees, you must wait the items are on the ground to collect them, and you must walk on them to pick them (or touch them with the boomerang). The disappears after a short time, if you delay to pick them.

Here are the different items you can get, and their effects :

  • The heart : It hovers in the airs before landing. It will restore 5% of your total life.

  • The magic flask : It will restore 5% of your total power.

  • The arrow : It rotates before landing. Your arrows stock increases by one.

  • The bomb : Your bombs stock increases by one.

The two first items can be generated since the items are activated. For the two others, they're generated only if you haven't set infinite stocks of bombs and/or arrows, and if you haven't set the maximum stocks of bombs and/or arrows on zero.

When you hit an opponent, you have :

  • A 75% chance to have between 1 and 3 rupees,

  • A 25% chance to have an item.

If you get an item, then you have :

  • A 25% chance to have any item if all are available,

  • A 33% chance to have any item if only 3 are available,

  • A 50% chance to have a heart or a magic flask if the two other items are disabled.

Note that if the rupees are disabled and the items are enabled (cf. Customization), then, items will be generated on each hit (the probabilities to get each item remains the same).
