~ BASIC OBJECTS ~

One of the Link for Mugen's great features is that, as in Zelda games, you can make your character evolves as you progress in the game. In order to do that, you have the possibility to buy objects when you win matches (see the "Shop" section).

The idea is quite simple : at the beginning of the game (according to your Customization parameters), Link starts without any object. Then, from the second match, you can access to the shop (if activated) to buy new objects.

Objects have two main goals : allowing you to use new moves on the one hand, and allowing you to buy another objects, on the other hands.

The number of arrows and bombs you can carry, and the number of arrows and bombs you get at each purchase can be set in the customization file (cf. the Customization section for more details). If you buy more bombs or arrows than you need, the overage will be lost. Your stocks of arrow and bombs are always displayed at the bottom of the screen, on the side, with your rupees' info :

Arrows are as well for the normal arrrows, for poisonned arrows and for homing device arrows (for these particular arrows, you must own the "poisonned arrows" and "homing device arrows" objects, respectively).

Bombs are as well for the normal bombs and for the time bombs (for these ones, you must own the hylian symbol).

When you reach the maximum of rupees and/or arrows and/or bombs, the information turns into green, to show you that you can't carry more of these items.

Note that the numbers of arrows and/or bombs aren't dislayed in three cases :

  • If you set the maximum of arrows and/or bombs to 0

  • If you activate the infinite arrows and/or bombs

  • If you willingly deactivate their displaying

These three cases are set in the Customization file.
