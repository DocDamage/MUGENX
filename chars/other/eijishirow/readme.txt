7-[CHARACTER'S BACKGROUND INFORMATIONS]
Name:		Eiji Kazeyama(Shirow)
Birthplace:     Japan
Birth date:   	December 29,1980(21 yrs old)
Height:        	1.89 m
Weight:        	71 kg  
Blood type:     0
Hobbies:        Collecting knives
Favourite food: Meat
Favourite music:Black Metal
Best sport:     Soccer
Most Important: His old scarf(it was Megumi's)
Most Unpleasant:Having to bear such loneliness
Likes:          His friend Jirai,his dead girlfriend(At least,she's supposed to be dead...)
Dislikes:       Being pushed around,having his knives collection being scattered around the room(it happened,really!)
Fighting style: Compilation of various fighting styles


Eiji is the firstborn of the boss of the Kazeyama family,an high-ranked family in the mafia hierarchy.
His mother was killed by his father,Kazuya,after she tried to sell him to the police.Kazuya decided 
anyway to grow Eiji,even if he hated him.He kept accusing him of his mother's death,saying that she 
died after giving him to the light,to let him grow angst so he could be controlled better. Eiji grew 
up as an angst-filled teen,and,after having found some evidence that it wasn't his fault if his mother 
died.He talked about this with his father,and the only thing he obtained was his father's anger,and the 
decision to leave the clan and live a normal life.

Fate was very fast in finding him:That night,he was almost ran over by a car.He then proceeded to 
beat up the driver for being such careless,but it turned out to be a red-haired young woman,that 
quickly apologized to him,and asked him to drive her car to the hospital,as she wasn't such a good 
driver and her brother was feeling very sick.He was going to refuse,but he changed his mind as she started
crying.
At the hospital,she introduced herself as Megumi Shinigami,and asked for the name of her saviour.Eiji knew
that his surname had a bad name,outside the mafia,and he decided to rename himself as his favourite comic artist:
he then renamed himself as Eiji Shirow.

Eiji and Megumi ended up together,and Eiji became the best friend of Rikard,Megumi's younger brother.He owed a lot
to both Megumi and Rikard,and he then decided to steal some money from his father in order to lend them an hand.
He was leaving from the house with an huge amount of money,but he accidentally heard a conversation via phone occuring 
between his father and one of his subordinates.He then had no doubt:It was him,who killed his mother!In a burst of rage,
he jumped on his father's neck and started choking him,but he easily escaped the grab and pointed a gun at him.
'Yes,I killed your mother',he said,'and I regret I didn't kill you too.Well,I can still do it now,don't you think?'.
He was both scared and enraged at the same moment,but he felt something different inside his body.He quickly disarmed
his father,but when he threw the gun away,it quickly exploded.He then looked at his hands:both of them were burning in a
red flame!
He didn't know where that power came from,but he quickly found an use to it:he burned his father to death,and set on fire
the manor while escaping.
The next day,he gave all the money he stole to Megumi,telling her that his father died and this was what he left to him.


The medical operations on Rikard were successful,and the three of them (Eiji,Megumi and Rikard) decided to go on a field
trip.Rikard ran out of energy,saying that he still wasn't used to such long walks.Eiji decided to stay with him,as Megumi
ran towards the car.After some minutes,all of a sudden Rikard twitched,got up,and started running towards his sister.Eiji
ran behind him.
They didn't found Megumi,but a middle-aged man with a killer look on his eyes,that Eiji quickly recognized:Tetsuya Kirazaki,that proceeded to explain that Megumi was no more,and that he was here to kill both Eiji and Rikard.Then he
proceeded to kick a shocked Rikard on the ground,but Eiji stopped him and the two started fighting.Eiji was scared of
showing his powers to Rikard,but his friend quickly suprised him,as he ran forwards Tetsuya,grabbed his head and
pushed him against a tree.Then,a flash of light appeared,and when it was gone,Rikard let Tetsuya fall on the ground,
showing his butchered head.He looked at his blood stained hand for a while,then he asked Eiji,screaming,who was that man
and what was happening.Eiji,after realizing that Megumi was gone,told Rikard that Tetsuya was one of the top-ranked
henchmen of his father,and then proceeded to explain him his story.

Rikard didn't take it too well:He blamed Eiji for what happened that day,and ran away.Eiji was alone,again,his newfound
life shattered in a few seconds...his girlfriend was dead,his closest friend killed a person...
he decided that he was done for good.Some days later,he went to the train station to leave the city....

...but,before he was able to take a train,he noticed a weird,cape wearing man young man who was looking at him.He didn't
even bother to talk to him,and that man quickly killed two people near to him.Police came to stop him,but all of them were
killed in a few seconds.Then,the man introduced himself as Igniz,and asked Eiji to join an unknown organization called
NESTS.Eiji didn't want to,but Igniz came out with a good argumentation:Megumi.He explained that if Eiji joined him,he'd
create a clone of Megumi just for him.Eiji didn't even think twice:he joined Igniz and the two of them left the station.
He became an high-ranked agent,and was dispatched in Jirai's squad.Between the two of them began a close rivalry,that quickly
turned into friendship.

2 years later,he was assigned to a mission:watch over a martial arts tournament,and kidnap the winner in order to bring him
to NESTS.It didn't take too long for him to realize:his old friend,Rikard,was fighting the finals!He was a bit surprised
by the fact that he became a such skilled fighter,but didn't care so much.Then,in the final battle,a man showed up,
introducing himself as Insidum Galactica,and asked the finalists to join him,or die.Even if the other finalist,Val Vain,was
going to surrender to Insidum,Rikard didn't,and the 2 of them fought for a long time.Rikard quickly took the upper hand,but
a careless move opened him to Insidum's attacks.Right before Insidum landed the final blow,Eiji interrupted the attack and
disposed of him in a brutal way.
Rikard was NOT happy to see Eiji,and tried to fight him,but he was very tired and quickly fell under his blows.Eiji then
finished the mission,by taking Rikard to NESTS.

Later that day,the local NESTS base had an accident,where both Rikard and Megumi escaped.Eiji didn't know about Megumi's
presence,but he later found it out when hacking into some files in order to get info about the incident.He then found 
out that it wasn't a clone,it was the REAL Megumi,and the experiments she was on were terrible.He decided to 
run away in order to find her,but before doing that he decided to kill Igniz,for everything he ended up doing 
for him.

But he was stopped by Jirai.
'you're going nowhere,son.'
'son my ass!You,ALL of you fooled me!Thinking I was looking to a clone,while you were holding my girlfriend captive!I'm not
going to let you pass....'
'you can't fight Igniz.He'll kill you!'
'Why do you care?After all,I'm nothing but a toy to you,isn't it?'
'You can't understand.Run away,Eiji.Go,find her!'
'After I'm done with you...'

He was quickly beaten by Jirai.His friend decided anyway to let him go,and report to Igniz that he was killed in action.He
then brought an unconscious Eiji away from the base,and left him a note that reported about his decision.


When Eiji woke up,he read the note,and realized what actually happened.

This was going to be his final mission.



8-[MOVELIST]

-KOF-TYPE GAMEPLAY MOVES-

-Power Build- 
hold A+B
*Your tipical power-up motion.You can stock up to 3 powers levels this way.

-Dodge roll-
A+X or Z
*You can dodge opponent's attack this way.Can go backwards if you hold back while dodging.

-Knockdown attack-
B+Y or C
*An attack that knockdowns the opponent.(duh!)

-Guard Cancel-
/Need 1000 power
Execute a Dodge Roll or a Knockdown attack while stand-crouch guarding
*you can counter an attack,either by dodging it or knocking the opponent down.


-COMMAND ATTACKS-
None. He causes enough damage without them.

-SPECIAL ATTACKS-

-Darkness Sweep Kai-
-QCF+P
One of Kyo/Iori's special moves, remodeled by Eiji. You can hold the punch keys to hold the fireball.

-Clipping Chimera Kai-
-QCB+P
Flaming uppercut. Again, this move is Kyo's.

-Cold Sun-
-DP+P
Your usual rising uppercut thing. Kinda looks like Andy's Sho Ryu Dan.

-Mirror Illusion-
QCB+K
Eiji warps himself forward a little. In the first frames of the animation, he has autoguard.

-Meteor Strike-
QCB+K, in the air
Eiji thrusts himself down to the ground with a flaming kick.

-Fallen Leaf combination
QCF+K -> QCB+K -> F+A
     \> QCF+P  \> QCB+P
A simple combo attack you can chain into supers. Eiji does different attacks depending on the motion you execute.

-DESPERATION MOVES-
(need 1000 power)

-Blazing Flash-
QCF,QCF+P
Eiji dashes forward, hits the opponent and delivers a flame pillar while standing behind them.

-Burn-
QCB,QCB+P
Eiji juggles the opponent, and hits them on the way back with a blazing fist attack. If you execute QCB+P during the attack,
he'll use a flame pillar follow-up.

-Saber Beam-
F,HCF+P
Eiji charges up and shoots 3 flame beams from his hand. Can be holden up.


-SUPER DESPERATION MOVES-
(need 2000 power)

-Blazing Flash-
QCF,QCF+P
Eiji dashes forward, hits the opponent and delivers a flame pillar while standing behind them. It deals more hits than the DM version,and, of course, more damage.

-Saber Beam-
F,HCF+P
Eiji charges up and shoots more flame beams from his hand.


-HIDDEN SUPER DESPERATION MOVES-
(need 2000 power and life < 300)

-Furious Dragon Breath-
QCB+All of the attack keys -OR- QCB+z+c
Eiji grabs the opponent, delivers them a massive amount of standing punches, juggles him into air, slams him down and proceed to finish the attack with a flaming dragon.
