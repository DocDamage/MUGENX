LILITH AENSLAND
v1.3 for MUGEN 2001.04.14

Made by Baby Bonnie Hood (bbhood21@yahoo.com)
Proudly made in the Philippines. (Mabuhay ang mga Pinoy!!)


BASIC AND NORMAL MOVES:

Dash              - F,F
Retreat           - B,B
Hop Jump          - lightly tap U
Long Jump         - D,U
Air Recover       - PP in the air
Advancing Guard   - P,P,P,P/K,K,K,K while blocking on the ground
Moving Get-up     - hold B/F when down
Honey Whisper     - LP
Neck Cherish      - LP while close
Perplex Touch     - D + LP
Little Gimlet     - LP in the air (hits high)
Funny Sweep       - LK
Soothing Tip      - D + LK (hits low)
Hard Point        - LK in the air (hits high)
Guilty Silhouette - MP
Heart Piercing    - MP while close
Evil Wing         - D + MP
Surprise Edge     - MP in the air (hits high)
Leg Impulse       - MK
Sensual Insert    - D + MK (hits low)
Coquettish Line   - MK in the air (hits high)
Splash Libido     - HP
Ecstasy Climb     - HP while close
Mysterious Arc    - D + HP
Drastic Oval      - HP in the air (hits high)
Ruddy Wand Opener - HK (hits high)
Necro Desire      - HK while close
Romantic Slay     - D + HK (hits low)
Lyrical Tear      - HK in the air (hits high)

THROWS:

Innocent Hug  - B/F + MP/HP while close
Childish Drop - B/F + MP/HP while close in the air

SPECIAL MOVES: (ES requires 1 power level)

Soul Flash    - D,DF,F + P
Shining Blade - F,D,DF + P (can be used while blocking)
Merry Turn    - D,DB,B + K
Mystic Arrow  - F,D,B + P (unblockable)
Toe Pierce    - U + P/K while enemy is down

SUPER MOVES: (requires 1 power level unless otherwise stated)

Splendor Love      - F,D,DF + KK
Luminous Illusion  - LP,LP,F,LK,HP
Gloomy Puppet Show - B,D,F + KK (PalNo 1-6 only, requires 2 power levels)
Brilliant Shower   - MP,LP,B,LK,MK (PalNo 7-12 only, requires 2 power levels)
Radiant Arrow      - F,HP,MP,LP,F (unblockable, requires 3 power levels)
Mindless Doll      - LP,LP,F,MP,HP
