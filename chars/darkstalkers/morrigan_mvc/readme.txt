  Controls
  ---------------------------------------

    Basic Standing Attacks
    ---------------------------------------
      X              - Weak Punch
      Y              - Medium Punch (Launcher)
      Z              - Strong Punch
      A              - Weak Kick
      B              - Medium Kick
      C              - Strong Kick

    Basic Crouching Attacks
    ---------------------------------------
      X             - Low Weak Punch
      Y             - Low Medium Punch
      Z             - Uppercut
      A             - Low Weak Kick
      B             - Low Medium Kick
      C             - Sweep Kick

    Basic Air Attacks
    ---------------------------------------
      X             - Air Weak Punch
      Y             - Air Medium Punch
      Z             - Air Strong Punch
      A             - Air Weak Kick
      B             - Air Medium Kick
      C             - Air Strong Kick
      U + C         - Axe Kick

    Throws
    ---------------------------------------
      F/B + y or z (when close) - Throw A
      F/B + b or c (when close) - Throw B

    Special Attacks
    ---------------------------------------
      D, DF, F + Punch          - Soul Fist
      DF, D, F + Punch          - Shadow Blade
      F, DF, D, DB, B + Punch   - Vector Drain

    Hyper Attacks
    ---------------------------------------
      D, DF, F + 2Punches       - Soul Eraser
      DF, D, F + 2Punches       - Silhouette Blade
      D, DF, F + 2Kicks         - Darkness Illusion
      x, b, B, y, c             - Eternal Slumber
