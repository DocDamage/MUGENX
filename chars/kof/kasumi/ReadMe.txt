 Installation
+++++++++++++++
 DownLoad 1 file named "Kasumi_0101.zip" or "Kasumi_0731.zip".
 The "Kasumi_????.zip"  contains 22 files shown below.
  Kasumi.air		Kasumi7.act
  Kasumi.cmd		Kasumi8.act
  Kasumi.cns		Kasumi9.act
  Kasumi.def		Kasumi10.act
  Kasumi.sff		Kasumi11.act
  Kasumi1.act		Kasumi12.act
  Kasumi2.act		Kasumi.snd
  Kasumi3.act		ReadMe_J.txt (In Japanese)
  Kasumi4.act		ReadMe.txt (In English, this file)
  Kasumi5.act		Skill.zip (Containes commands)
  Kasumi6.act		Kasumi2.cns (For 0627 cns file)

 If you still use Mugen 0627 , rename "Kasumi2.cns" to "Kasumi.cns".
 
 Unzip a file then, put these files into 1 folder named "Kasumi".
 Then Put "Kasumi" folder into your Mugen "charas" folder.

 Ex.)
   muk00101-----charas
              :    |____Kasumi
              :    :       |____Kasumi.air
              :    :       :
              :    :       :
              :    :       :

 Add this line in your "select.cfg" (or "select.def")in "data" folder.

 Kasumi, stages/******.def

 Replace ****** with your favorite background name.

 Ok, run your Mugen then the Kasumi's face is added your character
 select screen. Let's enjoy !!!


++++++++++++++++
 Commands
++++++++++++++++
 Unzip "Skill.zip".
 Please show "Skill_E.htm" in the "Skill" folder.
 Button layout is below...

  X Y Z
 (l ; :) : Deffault Keys
  A B C
 (, . /) : Deffault Keys

 X...Light Punch  ,  Y...Hard Punch  ,  Z...Recover
 A...Light Kick   ,  B...Hard Kick   .  C...Extra Hard Attack

++++++++++++++++++
 Featuer
++++++++++++++++++
 / Air recover or fall recover
   Press X+A or Z Button , when she can recover.

 / Quick forward or backward
   Press X+A or Z Button on the ground.

 / Guard cancel quick forward or backward (Costs half power gauge)
   Press X+A or Z Button , when she guarded.

 / Guard cancel Extra Attack (Costs half power gauge)
   Press Y+B or C Button , when she guarded.

 / Chain Combo
   Light P -> Light K -> Hard P -> Hard K

 / She also has 2 Level 3 super Arts made by me(M.M.R.).
