-- What's this? --

This is a CNS "patch" for people who have already downloaded Shermie from
The king of fighters '97 & '98, adapted for play with Elecbyte's
fighting game engine M.U.G.E.N. by me, NeoGouki (gouki@hem.passagen.se).

-- What's needed? --

The original build of Shermie. If you haven't downloaded it you have no use for this patch.

-- What's new? --

* Added some some EnvShake to the Shermie whip (thanks Sunboy).
* Made the Shermie stand / Kourai push the opponent further back.
* Added "edge" to all of Shermie's throws to make them look better when in a corner.
