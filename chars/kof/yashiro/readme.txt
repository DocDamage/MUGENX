---------------------------------------------------------------
1.0 Introduction
---------------------------------------------------------------
 First of all, Yashiro having the ability to transform was an
 idea taken (..stolen?! >:P) from NeoGouki's Chris. Anyways,
 this Yashiro is mainly based on KOF98. When he change to his
 'Orochi Form' he has the attacks of KOF97 and the specials of
 KOF98.

4.0 Move List
---------------------------------------------------------------
	x = not inplented in yet
-=[4.1 Both Form]=-

- Weak Punch
	X
- Heavy Punch
	Y
- Weak Kick
	A
- Heavy Kick (Overhead Priority)
	B
- Elbow Slam
	F + X
- Low Side Kick
	F + B
- Gut Punch
	(close) F + Y
- Throat Grab & Slam
	(close) F + B

-=[4.2 Normal Form]=-

* Orochi no Chikara Mezamer (Level 2)
	Start Button
* Million Bash Stream (Level 1 DM, Level 3 SDM)
	D, DB, B, DB, D, DF, F + X or Y (tap Punch to continue)
* Final Impact (Level 1 DM, Level 3 SDM)
	D, DF, F, D, DF, F + X or Y (hold to power up)
- Duel Upper
	F, D, DF + X or Y
- Sledgehammer
	D, DB, B + A or B
- Mighty Missle Bash
	F, DF, D, DB, B + X or Y
- Jet Counter
	B, DB, D, DF, F + X or Y
- Jet Finisher
	after Jet Counter, D, DF, F + X or Y

-=[4.3 Orochi Form]=-

* Revert back to Normal Form
	Start Button
*Ankoku Jigoku Gokuraku Otoshi (Darkness Hell Paradise Drop)
	(close) F, DF, D, DB, B, F, DF, D, DB, B + X or Y (Y: being worked on)
x*Araburu Daichi (Wild Earth Warrior)
	D, DF, F, D, DF, F + A or B
x*Hoeru Daichi (Howling Earth)
	D, DF, F, D, DF, F + X or Y (hold to power up)
- Niragu Daichi (Authority of Earth)
	(close) F, DF, D, DB, B, F + X or Y
- Odoru Daichi (Dancing Earth)
	(close) B, DB, D, DF, F + X or Y
- Musebu Daichi (Smothering Earth)
	B, DB, D, DF, F + A or B
- Kujiku Daichi (Wrenching Earth)
	D, DB, B + X or Y (does not strike)
