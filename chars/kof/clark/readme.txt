Legend
======

|A|     = Light Punch
|B|     = Light Kick
|C|     = Heavy Punch
|D|     = Heavy Kick
|START| = Taunt


Basic Command
=============

When selecting Clark,hold |Start| = Kof2000 Fighting Style
|A|+|B|                           = Dodge Slide
Hold Back, |A|+|B|                = Dodge Back
|A|+|B| while blocking            = Max Slide,Comsume 1 Power Stock
|A|,|B|,|C| or |D| while sliding  = Slide Attack 
|C|+|D|                           = Body Toss Attack or Knock Down
|C|+|D| while blocking            = CD Counter,Comsume 1 Power Stock
Forward, Forward		  = Run
Back, Back			  = Step Back
While running, press up		  = Hyper Jump
Press Down then up		  = Hyper Jump
Tap up				  = Low Jump
|A|+|B|+|C|			  = Counter Mode,Comsume 3 Power Stock
|B|+|C|+|D|			  = Armor Mode,Comsume 3 Power Stock
|B|+|C|				  = Summon Ralf Striker,Comsume 1 Power Stock
With Ralf Striker around,|B|+|C|  = Self Striker
Low Life			  = SDM Mode(Kof99 mode)


Armor Mode:
===========

Press BCD (3 Stocks)
This mode lasts around 8 seconds.
You character flashes GOLD during the time limit.

You will have HYPER ARMOR during this mode which means:
-You will not stagger nor fall when hit by most (ground) attacks
-Attacks inflict more damage
-You have no DMs
-Slide Attacks knock down


Counter Mode:
=============

Press ABC (3 Stocks)
This mode lasts around 20 seconds.
Your character will flash RED during it's activity.

You will have:
-Unlimited DMs, DMs won't have pause
-Attacks inflict more damage
-Gatling Attack & stomping can be canceled into DM's
-Slide Attacks are cancel-able


Fighting Style
==============
	Some people prefer Kof99 fighting style,some prefer Kof2000 one.This Clark 
include both.Clark is default to play in Kof99 mode.To play him in Kof2000 mode,
hold |START| while selecting him.


What not in Kof99 mode compare to arcade:
-----------------------------------------
- At the time being guard crush,no guard crush font will be shown
- No striker icon and stock
- Striker does not comsume striker stock,instead it consume 1 power stock
- During Armor & Counter mode,no time bar is shown
- Doing Taunt will not bring opponent forward


Whats in Kof2000 mode compare to Kof99 mode:
--------------------------------------------
- SDM will not be perform at low health,instead new introduce "MAX ATTACK"
- New edit spark for DMs
- Active Striker Introduce
- Self Striker Introduce
- Background color invert when performing DM.


Strikers
========
	For Kof99,striker can be summon when on land with control.For Kof2000,you can
summon anytime except when been hit.Summon striker in both style consume 1 power
stock.To summon striker,press |B|+|C|.

Self Striker(Newly Introduce,not in arcade Kof2000)
---------------------------------------------------
	Only avaliable in kof2000 fighting style.When Striker is been summon out,pressing
|B|+|C| again will peform Self Striker(No Power stock consume).This is useful to perform
striker chains & make the striker system better.

Tips
====

     Some people think grabber like Clark is difficult to use or control.They
have difficulties on doing a HCFX2 command when near opponent,as they panic when
opponent like iori, trap them in corner.Here are some tips to use clark in mugen
(this clark) & arcade.Some of them work in other characters.

     Clark have an unique close 2 hits strong punch.I can say this punch attack is
very useful, as it can easily chain up moves after the 1st hits.Try all these in training
mode.I am sure you can find Clark interesting!

2 Hits Ultra Argentina BackBreaker(UAB)
---------------------------------------
Near opponent, HCB+C then quickly after the 1st hits,do a HCB+A/C.If successful, Clark
will use the 2 hits strong punch. After the 1st hits of the punch,it will cancel to
Ultra Argentina BackBreaker(UAB)! Difficulty to learn : **

Running Super Argentina BackBreaker(SAB)
----------------------------------------
If you are quite near to your opponent(a distance where clark far heavy punch cannot 
reach & hit).Tap fwd,fwd & HCF + Kick.If successful,clark will run forward a small 
distance and do a successful Super Argentina BackBreaker(SAB), provided the opponent
is on the ground.Took me a few minutes to master! Difficulty to learn : *

Running Ultra Argentina BackBreaker(UAB)
----------------------------------------
Condition is the same as Running Super Argentina BackBreaker(SAB).Do a HCB first,then
quickly tap fwd,fwd & HCB + Punch.If successful,clark will run fwd a small distance
and do a Ultra Argentina BackBreaker(UAB), provided the opponent is on the ground.
Took me quite some time to do it.The best is when you are knock down to the ground
lying down,the opponent is quite near you waiting for you to getup.Just at the
moment when you are getting up,Do a HCB first,when fully getup then quickly tap 
fwd,fwd & HCB + Punch.You will getup and rush forward to give your opponent a suprise!
Difficulty to learn : ****

Fake Flashing Elbow
-------------------
This move is enable from 98 onwards.After Super Argentina BackBreaker(SAB),wait for 1
second, then do a flashing elbow(QCF + Punch).When successful,the flashing elbow will
miss the opponent & clark will getup at the back of the opponent.If the opponent is blur
by what you are doing,he will just stand down there(which i had been doing before ~.~).
Quickly do another SAB or perhaps UAB with a power stock provided.But remember that you
can be hit when getting up, it is a 50% risk and don't use it too often. Difficulty 
to learn : *

Hold button trick
-----------------
Actually this is a trick applicable to all the kof(arcade) character.I had equipped
this clark with it,so feel free to use it.Here is the trick. You may find it very 
difficult to link the 2 hits near heavy punch with running three.With this trick, you
can! According to the above 2 Hits Ultra Argentina BackBreaker(UAB) method,you will do
a HCF+C,to link it,but you will find once you access HCF+C,you will end up doing Mount 
Tackle .Now, hold button B(which is an unuse light kick button),then do a HCF+C,release 
all the button then do HCF+B/D.If successful, you will notice that you can link up a
2 hits near strong punch with Running Three! Difficulty to learn : **

Bugs Issuse

to remove it,open up "mugen/chars/clark/clark.cns",scroll until the bottom,look for 
[State -3, 2.1],with dizzy & guard crush mode comments label.Delete them,and you are 
done!
- When playing in team mode,somtimes sparks will not be place correctly,i am looking 
hard for this problem.
- When playing in team mode,flashing elbow may not works well.
- UAB & SAB may have trouble getting the opponent down,don't worry,clark will not
hang there.There will be self correction after a short time.
- Because of the active AI implementation,AI may still attack even when the match is
set.
- And some small error here and there when playing in team mode
