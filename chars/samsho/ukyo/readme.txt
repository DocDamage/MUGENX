*****************************
* OFFENSIVE COUNTER FEATURE *
*****************************

Like in the previous version of my Ukyo, you can perform
all kind of attacks during evading (XY). In this version you
can gain attack-power by evading an incoming attack and then
counter it with an attack by yourself. It's no matter what
kind of attack you choose: basics, specials and waza's will
work. A succesful counter-attack from the evade-stance will
rise your move's damage-value to 150%!



***********************************
* RAGE EXPLOSION AND FLASH ATTACK *
***********************************

Anytime during the fight, you can press XYZ simultanously to
let your power-bar (or rage-gauge for ss-chars) explode. The
screen gets dark after this move. While the background is black
you can press XYZ again to deliver a strong flash attack. The
damage of the flash-attack is mainly your life-bar reversed. So
remember: low lifebar -> big damage, full lifebar -> light damage
Also the time, the screen stays black, after the explosion depends
on your lifebar: low lifebar -> long time, full lifebar -> very
short time.

REMEMBER: once you got rid of your powerbar, you can't do waza's
for the rest of the whole match!
