installation
1. Unzip UKYO.ZIP into MUGEN\CHARS\UKYO\

2. edit MUGEN\DATA\SELECT.CFG and insert the following line:

ukyo, stages/flames.def

(FLAMES is just an example, insert a valid stage!)

whats different from the original?
1. much faster than the original
2. moves from bust, slash (SS1-4 and SS64 II)
3. no spider thrust special (cause its a dump move...)
4. additional moves (piercing moon, Reversal)
5. small combo ability
6. the jump-fwd-C is the same as the jump-up-C
