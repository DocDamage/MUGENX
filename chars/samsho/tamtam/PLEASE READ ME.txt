------------------------Gameplay Overview--------------------------------------------------------

TAM-TAM unlike most sword-swinging characters is prepared for ranged atacks. In a close-quarter
method, because his strong atacks are to be used away from the enemy and close atacks have the 
idea to push away the opponent or "deflect it" :) or in other words 
ranged combat.
also, unlike every big and muscular char you ever found TAMTAM has quick feet.
this char is perfect for rookies and begginers.
TAM TAM can´t make any cool combos yet. But, try chaining a super and light 
punch 2 twice or an air fireball with a medium punch

if you find any good combos please let me know


------------------------installation-------------------------------------------------------------

Add TAMTAM to your Select.def in the "DATA" folder of MUGEN.
Then, in the chars folder, name a new folder named "TAMTAM" and be sure that all the files in
both zip files are in that folder.
