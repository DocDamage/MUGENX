Y   ->  MEDIUM SLASH
Z   ->  STRONG SLASH

A   ->  KICK

XY  ->  EVADE

XYZ ->  POWER CHARGE

*****************************
* OFFENSIVE COUNTER FEATURE *
*****************************

Like in the previous version of my Hanzo, you can perform
all kind of attacks during evading (XY). In this version you
can gain attack-power by evading an incoming attack and then
counter it with an attack by yourself. It doesn't matter what
kind of attack you use: basics, specials and waza's will
work. A succesful counter-attack from the evade-stance will
rise your move's damage-value to 150%!



*******************************************
* THE FIVE SECRET SCROLLS OF THE IGA CLAN *
*******************************************

SCROLL ONE: KAJI (FIRE)

With Perfect Control of his Qi, Hanzo can create deadly
flames from his bare hands. He can send fire waves
across the battlefield and he can create powerful twisting
flames to roast his enemys. Be careful... and keep explosives
away from his flaming hand!


SCROLL TWO: MIZU (WATER)

Like the motion of water in a river, Hanzo can move his
arms to any vulnerable point of his opponents body and
like a rock in the stream, he can crush the bones inside
of it. Once captured in Hanzo's special bone-crushing
techniques, there is no escape.


SCROLL THREE: KAZE (WIND)

Hanzo can move as fast as the wind and he uses this technique
to quickly change the position on the battlefield. He can dodge
incoming attacks easily by disappearing in a cloud of smoke, just
to re-appear behind the enemy.


SCROLL FOUR: TOCHI (EARTH)

Like the fingers of a farmer who places seeds in the earth, Hanzo
can place explosives inside the enemy's body. Once placed inside
the body of an opponent, there is no way to get them out... keep away
from fire!


SCROLL FIVE: ??? (EMPTINESS)

This is the ultimate technique of Hattori Hanzo. By summon all forces of
space and time, Hanzo can override the forces of nature to become one with
the emptiness. In this state Hanzo is invisible!
