COMMAND:
=========

(see your keyboard the right part)
    x:Light slash                     7  8  9
    y:Medium slash                    4  5  6
    z:Hard slash                      1  2  3
    c:Kick        

 (N=NEAR,F=FAR,L=LIGHT,M=MEDIUM,H=HARD,K=KICK,
  S=STAND,C=CROUCH,J=JUMP,F=FORWARD,B=BACK,D=DASH,)
