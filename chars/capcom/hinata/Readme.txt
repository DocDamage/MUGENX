  Controls
  --------

    Basic Controls
    --------------
      D     - down
      F     - forward
      U     - up
      B     - back
      x     - weak punch
      y     - medium punch
      z     - strong punch
      a     - weak kick
      b     - medium kick
      c     - strong kick
      start - Taunt

      
    Basic Movements
    -----------------
      F,F                       - Run
      B,B                       - Back Step
      x+a                       - Roll
      x+a, during guard         - Guard Cancel - Roll(needs 1 power bar)
      y+b                       - Dodge
      any buttom during dodge   - Dodge Attack
      y+b, during guard         - Guard Cancel - Attack(needs 1 power bar)
      x+a, when knocked down    - Safe Fall
      z+a                       - Charge
      

    Normal Attacks
    ---------------
      DF + c (Launcer)
      F + b (Overhead)
      in the air, D + y
      in the air, D + c
            
    
    Special Attacks
    ---------------
      Rengekiken: D, DF, F + x or y or z
      Rengekiken Addon: Tap Punch after Rengekiken (only medium and strong versions)                    Shouyouken: F, D, DF + x or y or z
      Enbukyaku: D, DB, B + a or b or c
      Kuuchuu Enbukyaku: in the air, D, DB, B + a or b or c
      Renyakudan: in the air, D, DF, F + a or b or c
      Renyakudan Addon: During Renyakudan, a or b or c
                
     
    Hyper Attacks (Level 1)
    -------------
      Hisho Rengekiken: D, DF, F, D, DF, F + x or y or z
      Air Buster: D, DB, B, D, DB, B + x or y or z
      Rassen Enbukyaku: D, DB, B, D, DB, B + a or b or c
      Kuuchuu Rassen Enbukyaku: in the air, D, DB, B, D, DB, B + a or b or c
      
    Hyper Attacks (Level 2)
    -------------
      Itadaki Scoop (Ran Team Up): D, DF, F + xa
      Double Kiaidan (Batsu Team Up): D, DF, F + yb 
     
    
    Combo Notes
    ------------

      1. Jump with any attack -> Strong Punch -> Hisho Rengekiken Level 2 -> Any Hyper Level 1;
 
      2. Jump with any attack -> Strong Punch -> Rassen Enbukyaku Level 2 (5 hits) -> Rassen Enbukyaku Level 1;

      3. Jump with any attack -> Strong Punch -> Air Buster (4 hits) -> Rassen Enbukyaku Level 1;

      4. Jump with any attack -> Crouch Light Kick/Punch (x4) -> Light Rengekiken

      5. Jump with any attack -> Strong Punch -> Any Special or Hyper Attacky Hyper Attack

      6. Launcer -> Super Jump -> Weak Punch -> Weak Kick -> Medium Punch -> Medium Kick -> Strong Punch -> Strong Kick
    
      7. Jump with any attack -> Launcer -> Super Jump -> Weak Punch -> Weak Kick -> Medium Punch -> Medium Kick -> Kuchuu Rassen Enbukyaku Level 2 (5 hits) -> Rassen Enbukyaku Level 1; 
     

    Notes
    ------------ 

      1. Hypers Level 2 can be canceled into hypers level 1 (like in cvs2 C-Groove);
      
      2. Light Rengekiken is a simple Projectile;
  
      3. After Launcing Hinata can make an air combo or connect Shoyouken or any hyper;
