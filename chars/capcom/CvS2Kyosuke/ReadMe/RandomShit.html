鏡恭介

Some Basic Fighting Notes to get started with Kagami Kyôsuke

    The GenEi kick is normally wide-open after the actual kick but the EX moves available for chaining after it can be used to lure the opponent.
    The Cross Cutter is normally useful mainly at non-long range because of its unusual trajectory (your opponent usually find out too late that he can't jump above it to counter Kyôsuke) but the EX version allows it to keep up against a basic fireball user : one energy burst will stop the fireball and the 2 others can hit the opponent.
    The Raijin Upper is more of a defense move though the start-up is a bit slow, making it hard to use it fast), but the EX version can be used to suprise the opponent from a greater distance.

    Basically, Kyôsuke is more of a close-range fighter, with some quick moves and short ground combos, varied enough to trap the opponent - a few of his basic moves have a good range, too. His aerial mobility is way above average as he can perform advanced air combos (comboability is similar to when he's on the ground) with many attacks that can be used to counter a jumping-in opponent. His GenEi Breaker allows him to keep up with opponents who try to keep him at a distance with fireballs, though it might be too slow.
    Kyôsuke's main strength is basically his mobility and his ability to vary attacks and counter-attacks at a varying speed to surprise the opponent.
    Well, against a human opponent, that is... Against the computer, his GenEi kick and its EX chains will still be his main weapon, interrupted by no many Cross Cutters with EX variations. Also remember that Kyôsuke is excellent at aerial fighting.

    His last weapon is his launcher kick, which sends the opponent in the air and allows Kyôsuke to jump after him if it hits. Since Kyôsuke has a good  air comboability, don't hesitate... The heavy drawback is, its range is a pure joke, and the overall move is a bit open to counter-attacks. It cannot be comboed into, of course. Though, it can be found useful against opponents who try to pressure you.
Combo suggestion starting with the launcher :
    launcher, jump, LP, LK, HK, kuuchuu GenEi kick, EX GenEi kick / EX GenEi Wave.
    launcher, jump, LP, HP, HK, GenEi Wave, EX GenEi kick.
... I finally added the Tardy Counter, and I found out that it really gives a great opportunity for the launcher : if you're cornered and your opponent is pummeling your defence, Tardy counter into the launcher and jump for your best launcher-air combo.

Kagami Kyôsuke

RIVAL SCHOOLS : UNITED BY FATE

    The now deceased Imawano Mugen was a member of the Imawano ninja clan, like his brother Imawano Raizo. Mugen was a quite violent, evil man, and he had 2 sons : Kyôsuke and Hyô. Raizo, on the other hand, was quite the idealist.
Hyô, taking after his father, tried to raise some sort of army to take over Japan, then the world. For that, he first used his brainwashing skill on Raizo, who was trying to build a school - the Shiritsu Justice Gakuen (Justice private high school). Raizo originally wanted to educate students in order to prepare a better world, but Hyô's plan damaged that ideal.
    Kyôsuke, at that time, was more or less like his brother, though he was using his mother's family name, Kagami, because of his hatred for his father. However, that wasn't preventing him from lending his brother a hand. At the Taiyô Gakuen (Sun high school) where he was part of the discipline committee, he kidnapped a few people for Hyô, and ended up kidnapping Imawano Shizuku, Raizo's wife, who was a clerk at Taiyô Gakuen.
A few days later, a transfer student, Ichimonji Batsu, arrived and started investigating on those disparitions ; Kyôsuke kept an eye on him under the pretext of giving him a hand. Batsu turned up to be Raizo and Shizuku's son (Ichimonji was his mother's maiden name).
Teaming-up with various students from other schools, Batsu and Kyôsuke, along with Hinata, a girl from Batsu's class, ended up at the doors of Justice Gakuen. Kyôsuke, moved by Batsu's determination and by what the power of their friendship had allowed them to achieve, finally told everything to his friends and turned against his brother.
Eventually, after a fight, Kyôsuke managed to make Hyô understand his feelings, and Hyô finally abandonned his ideals, releasing Raizo and all of his victims from the brainwashing.

RIVAL SCHOOLS : PROJECT JUSTICE

   The Kirishima family had been working for the Imawano ninja clan since its creation. But with Hyô's failure, Kirishima Kurow decided that it was time for him to exterminate the latest heirs of the Imawano clan - that is, Kyôsuke, Raizo, Hyô and Batsu.
Kurow started by attempting a brainwash on Hyô ; he failed, but Hyô acted as if he had succeeded : Hyô, still wondering about his and Kyôsuke's new way, wanted to see if Kirishima's way was worthy enough, see if Kyôsuke's friendship could really help him. Kurow then also failed an assassination attempt on Raizo, but he succeeded in brainwashing Kazama Daigo, a powerful gang leader from another school.
That success turned up to be due to the only fact that Daigo's mind had already been damaged in the Rival Schools events : Daigo, secretly investigating on the kidnapping, had fought his way against Hyô all alone but was eventually surprised by Hyô's brainwashing attack, at a point where he thought he had won.
After that, Kurow started impersonating Batsu and attacking several students from other schools using that disguise to ruin Batsu's reputation.
    Kurow's plot was eventually revealed, and Kyôsuke, Batsu, Hinata and several of their friends teamed up against Kurow's allies. It was then Hyô decided that Kurow was not right after all, and turned his sword against a dumbstruck Kurow who realized that he had been tricked from the beginning ; Hyô almost killed him.
However, right at the end of the fight, Hyô's mind shattered : the evil spirit of his dead father, Mugen, which was sealed in Hyô's sword, took over Hyô's mind, turning him into an enemy known as Demon Hyô. The Taiyô Gakuen team fought against him, and in the end, Kyôsuke was crying, holding his dead brother's body in his arms.
    This is the last time Batsu and his friends saw Kyôsuke.

KAGAMI KYÔSUKE

   He first isn't very easy with people but begins to change when making friend with Batsu. Always quiet and serious, he sometime shows up being quite arrogant, you'll often see him fix his glasses in the middle of a fight... Afterall, this arrogance is not clearly missplaced - Kyôsuke really is a very skilled fighter, though not really strong in term of firepower.
His fighting style is not known as anything else than just "Kagami familial martial arts", he doesn't use anything of the Imawano ninja clan, from where his father and his brother are - though his GenEi kick is the same as Hyô's. You will have noticed that most of his skills revolve around the manipulation of lightnings and energy cuts.
