Some Basic Combos Notes to get started with Another Kyô

100 Shiki Oni Yaki can be "super canceled" : before Another Kyô starts leaping (between 1st and 2nd hit), perform any super move. This cancel has no malus.
75 Shiki Kai - modified can be chained into more or less anything.
Normal moves chains basically go that way : lp->lk->[hp OR hk], including crouch moves, you can skip any attack but not go backward ; then any normal move can be chained into a command move, then a special, a hyper, heavy normals into supers.
Also, close light moves can't be chained into far heavy moves. This system is close to Justice Gakuen's Textbook Combo. There's only the "Command Move" version of the Oboro Guruma that can't be chained into anything, naturally (he's in mid-air...)
Against fireball madness, use hcb plus the appropriate kick button to punish those cheap asses as fast as that.
Cancel the Kamijiri.Fukanzen into the Nettetsu when you have 3 power levels...
Hikigane and its 3 variations have some combos hidden. Heavy version (Gun Spark) can be chained into Kurodori (with tight command buffering), light and medium can be chained into Ara Gami, Doku Gami, Gun spark, light can be chained into Oni Yaki and most supers... (any super but the Kamijiri, actually)

Random shit for crazy guys

In japanese mythology, the number 108 has a particular meaning. You might have seen it other things like Saint Seiya (Hades' 108 Evil Stars, and Shaka's 108 beads rosary), in Gensô Suikoden (the 108 Heavenly Stars) or Shaman King (the 1080 beads rosary, for which the explaination is utterly idiot I might add... To multiply the power by 10, how stupid...) in japanimation, and surely other things, like martial arts and, last but not least, religion.
108 is the number of sins, and also the number of purifications for those sins. In Kyô's background, you see 2 of his techniques are called Sin Chant and Punishment Chant, the second following the first, that's not a coincidence. And his trademark moves, the Yami Barai and Orochinagi, are labelled 108. There. 
About the Oboro Guruma, I read somewhere that "pain follows the sin as the wheel follows the foot of the ox" (kuruma can mean wheel, car, chariot) so the wheel supposedly has that hidden meaning... Yeah, maybe. Here, Oboro Guruma is Haze Wheel. Maybe it's related in some way, maybe not, dunno.
Now for the Koto Tsuki.Yô, "yô" is the other pronounciation for "yang" (as in Yin and Yang), which is "positive" ; of course, Iori's version is Koto Tsuki.In, where "in" is "yin" ("negative"). I don't get the "koto moon" part, though (koto being a japanese harp). Plain poetry maybe, who knows.
I don't know anything about the other moves names/numbers.

About the "One of the Three Sacred Weapons" part.
The Sword is indeed one of the usual three treasures of the empire (I believe that one is from chinese mythology), along with the jewels (known as Magatama in Japan) and the mirror that reveals one's true nature. 
The Kusanagi no Tsurugi, Sword of Kusanagi (originally called Ame no MuraKumo no Tsurugi, Sword of the Clustering Rain Clouds) was found by Susano'o, god of War, Thunder and Oceans (enough already ?) inside the biggest mouth of the giant serpent Yamato no Orochi (Yamato being the ancient name of Japan, sometimes seen as some equivalent of the greek Gaea), which he had just slayed for fun. Susano'o was kinda "heat-tempered", though he wasn't too much of a bad guy, luckily ; and the giant snake Orochi was also rampaging through Japan, so there. 
Susano'o was brother to Amaterasu, goddess of Sun, and Tsukuyomi, god of the Moon. In SNK's storyline, that's where the emblems of Kusanagi and Yagami families are from. And so, in SNK's storyline, Kagura Chizuru, Yagami Iori and Kusanagi Kyô hold the 3 sacred weapons/treasures. 

Kyô versus Another Kyô

Another Kyô seems to be a lost clone who survived the destruction of NESTS. No one knows his purpose, his life or his way of thinking.

So this Another Kyô would rely a bit less on the flames of the Kusanagi clan and more on his fighting instinct, basically using more explosions. He looses some strenght in the process, but can use more raw power when needed. Weaker than the original Kyô but with instincts that can turn him somehow berserk when pissed off, some of the moves he kept from Kyô are a bit less powerful, but his highest level makes him look quite violent.
As he tweaked the Kusanagi arts of fighting, he gave up some moves, modified some others and made up the rest to fit his tastes.
