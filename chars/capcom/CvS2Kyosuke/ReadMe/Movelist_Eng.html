Some moves have an EX version  and eat 300 power away when used as such (SF3 feature)
EX versions of the moves can be used by pressing 2 attack buttons instead of one, according to the command (2 punch buttons or 2 kick buttons) - for example, the EX Raijin Upper is done by pressing forward, down, down-forward and 2 punch buttons. the EX GenEi kick is obtained by inputing a quarter circle forward and 2 kick buttons.
Basically, all special moves minus the Gen'Ei Breaker have an EX version available, and the details are listed in the movelist description, below the table.

Character informations

Name Kagami Kyôsuke (Capcom vs SNK 2 style)
Fighting style  
Combat notes Despite a movelist just a bit short, Kyôsuke is quite good at preventing the opponent from knowing where to attack, he has good counter moves, as well as some abilities for aerial fighting. Nobody jumps toward him and lives !

Details, some moves' secrets

Run, small jump, high jump, backward dash : Usual stuff, pretty self-explainatory.

 

Parry : Kyôsuke can parry anything if you tap forward at the right timing (less than 5 ticks before being hit). You can own any Messatsu Gô Hadô abuser easily with it. Kyôsuke glows blue (red if it was done while blocking) and recovers some life.
Zero Counter : If your opponent tries to eat your life away by forcing you to block, counter him away and own those aggressive bastards. With a punch button, Kyôsuke glows red while performing his light Gen'Ei kick - but can't do any of its follow-up. With a kick button, Kyôsuke performs his crouching heavy kick (and still glows red, yes). Also, it takes some of your powerbar too.
Tardy Counter : When you're finishing guarding a hit and should normally be recovering from the attack (meaning, still blocking but after the actual hit) you can perform any attack to break out of your guard and surprise your opponent. Well, the move keeps all of its up and down sides, so try not to do any stupid move.
For example, a GenEi Breaker performed as a Tardy Counter will jump above the opponent if done at close range (except if it's the light version), but if you're doing it while blocking a projectile, it has better chances to really surprise the opponent.
... This is a system taken from the Justice Gakuen serie, but might be a bit cheap in a 2D fighting game, I'm not sure.


Dodge : well, Kyôsuke can dodge anything during that sidestep / short forward or backward roll... Can also be canceled into any super.

[launcher] : Kyôsuke turns and does a reversed kick aimed at the opponent's chin. This kick sends the opponent in the air and by pressing up when the move connects, you can jump after him for an air combo. However, the range of this move is ridiculous. 
Probably, the best opportunity to use it would be as a Tardy Counter when you're cornered : if you time it well, it can rapidly become a deadly weapon to turn the game upside-down.
 

Cross Cutter : Kyôsuke creates a couple of energy bursts that eventually merge together. Can be used to catch someone who thinks he can jump over your fireball.
The EX version of the Cross Cutter creates a third energy burst that goes straight forward and can, as such, block basic fireballs.

GenEi Wave : While in the air, Kyôsuke stops, turns and create a small energy wave in front of him. Nothing too specific but actually quite useful.
The EX version of the GenEi Wave is (only) available during a GenEi kick : you can chain a GenEi kick into the EX GenEi Wave.

Raijin Upper : Kyôsuke waves his hand, creating a lightning rising from the ground. Quite a slow start-up (though the light version is much faster), but blocks 1-hit fireballs, and a good protection, Kyôsuke regains control before the end of the lightning. Very high range.
The EX version of the Raijin Upper is faster (as fast as the light version, to be more specific) and the lightning appears at about half a screen farther. It will not go off-screen, though, and if the screen edge is too close, the lightning will simply be a step before it and appear on-screen. 

GenEi Breaker : Kyôsuke leaps forward and smashes his opponent, striking at him with a lightning if it connects. Jumps over ground based fireballs.
The GenEi Breaker has no EX version.

GenEi kick : Quite the opposite of the Raijin Upper in term of purpose despite what it looks like, Kyôsuke does a fast somersault ; fast start-up, can counter many jumping-in attacks, but might be punished if missed. It can also be performed during a jump.
The EX version of the GenEi kick is an actual Double GenEi kick ; it can only be done during the ground version of the GenEi kick or during the GenEi Wave, and, much like the EX GenEi Wave, lets you chain a second GenEi kick by pressing qcf+2K.
I said that you could also perform it during the GenEi Wave, but of course, you can't if it already was the EX GenEi Wave : you can't chain both EXes indefinitely.

 
Kakusan Cross Cutter : Same as the normal Cross Cutter, with more bursts of energy.

Double GenEi kick : Actually a ground somersault followed by a GenEi kick and a GenEi Wave. Quite good to counter an opponent, you can also add a bonus GenEi kick ("tsuika GenEi kick", additionnal shadow kick) after the GenEi Wave by pressing qcf+K.
Super Raijin Upper : Basically, a wall made of lightning. The same as Raijin Upper, except that it does 4 to 8 hits. Same start-up speed, same recovery speed.
