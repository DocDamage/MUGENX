======================================================
Moves
======================================================

====================================
Hougeki Bakushin [Bombardment Dash]
====================================
D,DF,F, c

A dashing slash move that stuns the opponent for a limited time
that could only be followed by:

====================================
Nigenteki Katsu [Dual Split]
====================================
D,DF,F, b

A double slash technique that slams the opponent against the
edge of the screen.

====================================
Tei Houou Dan [Low Phoenix Bullet]
====================================
D,DF,F, x

A double cypher boomerang throw that hit's the opponent underneath
and retaliates for another shot at the back.
Unblockable if the opponent is "Standing", this move can be blocked
by Crouching.

====================================
Kou Houou Dan [High Phoenix Bullet]
====================================
D,DF,F, y
A double cypher boomerang throw that hit's the opponent above
and retaliates for another shot at the back.
Unblockable if the opponent is "Crouching", this move can be blocked
by Standing.

====================================
Densho Hou Dan [Homing Phoenix Bullet]
====================================
D,DF,F, z
A homing double cypher boomerang throw.
Need i explain more? :P

====================================
Aerial Houou Dan
====================================
D,DF,F, x/y/z
An aerial double cypher throw? Damn...

====================================
Wall Cling
====================================
D,DB,B, x/y/z
Clings to the wall. Can be done in mid-air and after Air-Recover.

====================================
Wall Kick
====================================
x/y/z (while in wall cling)
Performs a kick by taking momentum from the edge of the screen. eh?

====================================
Wall Cypher Throw
====================================
a/b/c (while in wall cling)
Performs a double cypher boomerang throw in wall cling mode.

====================================
Nightmare Shuriken
====================================
D,DB,B, a
Throws out 3 cypher shurikens that stuns the opponent so you
can beat the lights outta him...

Super Moves (yay!)
=======================================================
====================================

Level 1 Supers
====================================
Nigenteki Houou Dan [Dual Phoenix Bullet]
====================================
D,DF,F, y+z
Throws a double homing cypher throw. (2-4 Hits)

====================================
Sho Houou Dan [Rising Phoenix Bullet]
====================================
D,DB,B, b+c
Throws a hyper wcypher that takes the opponent into the air for
multiple damage. (10 Hits)

====================================
Shinku Houou Dan [Raging Phoenix Bullet]
====================================
D,DF,F, y+z (while in mid-air)
Throws yet another hyper wcypher (WTF? a rhyme! :P) that takes 
the player down and toward the edge of the screen. (10 Hits)


Level 2 Super
====================================
Kage Midare Giri [Shadow Berserker Slash]
====================================
D,DF,F, b+c (while in mid-air)
This is a mid-air super berserker combo type of move. (whoah)
(11 Hits.)


Level 3 Super
====================================
Guiding Light
====================================
D,DF,F, b+c (on the ground)
Hien generates an electrical charge to his cyphers then blast his 
opponent off till kingdom come. :P
(10 Hits)
