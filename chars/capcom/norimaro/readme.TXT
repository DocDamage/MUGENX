==============================================================================================

There are so many contradicting reports about his origin that I wont even go into them all
here.  Suffice it to say I'm fairly certain Norimaro is based on a real life Japanese comedian.

In Marvel Super Heroes Vs Street Fighter he is sort of an obsessed Otaku tourist who wants to
get pictures of all the fighters and some how ends up in the tournament.

USAGE
==============================================================================================

1 Get Mugen from Elecbyte at www.elecbyte.com

2 After setting up Mugen, click on the "chars" directory and create a new folder called
"norimaro". Unzip the files into this directory.

4 Open up SELECT.CFG (found in the "data" directory) and insert the following line
norimaro, stages/"background".def

..where "background" is the name of the background you wish to use with Norimaro.

5 Run Mugen

6 Enjoy

KNOWN ISSUES
==============================================================================================

In Marvel Super Heroes Vs Street Fighter there is a code that will allow Norimaro to air jump
up to 3 times.  The code, for those who are interested, is to hold the WK, MP, and HK buttons
while the match starts.

I've tried to do this in M.U.G.E.N. but I have been unsuccessful in my attempts to use a
variable to override the airjump.num parameter of the [Movement] portion of the cns.  It
would appear as if this is not possible right now.  I could be wrong.

To compromise I have made it so Norimaro can use 2 air jumps instead of 1 or 3.  That number
is right in the middle.  You could always edit the cns file to your liking if you aren't
happy with this.
