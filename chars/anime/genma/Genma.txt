WHAT´S NEW?
-----------

V3.0 (MUGEN00627):

- added missing sprites for the new mugen



V3.0:

- Rage-Explosion now visual for P1 and P2!

- Stand-C-Attack powered down... cant hit enemy while in
  the liedown-state.
  use the Mid-Air-C-Attack to deliver an additional hit to
  the your opponent on the ground.


V2.0:

- many minor bug-fixes

- new intro and win-sequence: the Panda-Dance ^_^

- Big Genma picture removed (it looks f´up in some
  stages...)

- Helper is now everytime during the match accessable and
  costs one powerbar (1000)

- Rage-Explosion (press A+B+X together with 3 Powerbars!)
  Until the powerbar is empty, every move does more damage
  and specials are more powerfull (and faster!)
  You will see a burning Rage-Symbol in the lower left corner if the
  conditions are met to bomb the gauge (but, until now, only with one
  Genma on Screen... did not figure out how to tell MUGEN to draw the
  symbol of the second player in the lower right...)!

- Anti-Master-Happosai-Strike SDM (during Rage-Explosion-Mode)
  D, DB, B, DB, D, DF, F + ab

- added two taunts
  press b+c (the taunts appear randomly)

- the ostrich-punch now can hit the enemy on the ground
  (perfect to deliver an additional hit to happosai, like
  in the manga!)



INSTALLATION:
-------------

1. Unzip GENMA.ZIP into MUGEN\CHARS\GENMA\

2. edit MUGEN\DATA\SELECT.CFG and insert the following line:

   genma, stages/flames.def

   (FLAMES is just an example, insert a valid stage!)



THE BUTTON SYSTEM: (4-button pad: a,b,c,d  6-Button pad: a,b,x,y)
---------------------------------

A            -  Quick Attack#1

B            -  Quick Attack#2
 
C            -  Fierce Attack#1

D            -  Fierce Attack#2


ADVANCED BASIC-MOVES:
---------------------

Assault Hop                  :  F, F

Escape Hop                   :  B, B

Mid-Air Recover (after hit!) :  a + b

Evade                        :  c + d

Rage-Gauge-Explosion         :  a + b + c (with Level 3 Power!)


THE SPECIAL MOVES:
------------------

Running Shoulder Ram         :  QCF  +  c

Leaping Head Butt            :  DP   +  d

Mid-Air-Roundhouse           :  QCB  +  c (after Head Butt!)

Leaping Roundhouse           :  QCB  +  c

Panda´s Rage (Shadow Boxing) :  QCB  +  d

Verbal Attack                :  QCB  +  b

Supersonic Punch             :  QCF  +  b

Supersonic Shadow Boxing     :  after Supersonic Punch (!) hold d

Combo Breaker                :  DB, D  + ab  (during guard!)

Saotome Strike               :  ab (while lying down! / Level 1 Power)

Power-Charge                 :  hold ab



DESPERATION MOVES:
------------------

Fathers Rage#1               :  QCB, HCF +  c

Fathers Rage#2               :  QCF, QCF +  c

Thousand Curses              :  F, HCF +  c



SUPER DESPERATION MOVE:
-----------------------

Rumiko Takahashi´s  Rage     :  QCB, HCF +  b
(Power-Level 3!!!)

Anti Master Happosai Strike  :  QCB, HCF +  ab
(only after Rage-Explosion while blinking)
