Movelist:
--------

Light Punch 	= X
Strong Punch 	= Y

Light Kick 	= A
Strong Kick	= B

Teleport	= Z
Charge		= C



Roll = A+X aka C(when power bar is on 3)

Counter = when guarding Z(uses 1 power bar)


Combos:


Punch -> fwd Punch -> fwd Punch
                  |
                  |-> fwd Kick -> up Punch

Kick -> fwd Kick -> back Kick -> back Punch


Kikoha can be done 3 times in a row.


TELEPORT ATTACKS: During a counter, you can follow it with other teleport attacks.
                  You require 500 of power (1/2 power bar).
                  Hold forward OR up OR down and press A+X.
                  Not that the DOWN version can't be followed.



Specials:
========


Elbow                = (jump) Down + Light Punch


Cho Oozaru Punch     = Fwd, Down, Fwd + Light Punch


Hishou Kyaku         = (jump) Down, Back + Light Kick


Surprise attack      = (wake up) HCB + Strong Kick


True Saiyan	     = D, F, DF, strong Kick


Ki Blast 1  	     = D, F, Light punch
Ki Blast 2	     = D, F, Strong Punch	
	Both can be done in the air
	When done on the grond, pressing the coresponding button twice will follow 	the first blast with 2 more.	

Hypers:
======


Big Bang Attack      = 2QCF 
(requires 1 Power bar)


Barage of Blasts     = 2QCF b


Final Flash          = 2QCB
(requires 3 Power bars)

Note: The Final Flash doesn't kill the opponent until the 3rd "Attack win"


Explosion            = HCB, HCB + Punch and Kikoha
(requires life less than 250/1000 - once per round)
